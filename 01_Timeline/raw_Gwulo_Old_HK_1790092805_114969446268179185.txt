<p><strong>General</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><a href="https://gwulo.com/node/62714"><em><strong>Courage in the Shadows</strong></em></a> is the newly published biography of <a href="https://gwulo.com/node/23634"><strong>Phyllis Nolasco Da Silva</strong></a>. Phyllis worked for the BAAG in Kwongchowwan for three dangerous years in WW2, controlling their agents there, and surviving interrogation and blackmail by the Japanese and their Chinese staff.<br />&nbsp;</li>
<li class="ck-list-marker-bold"><strong>Please help to put a Shamshuipo POW camp wartime diary online.</strong>
<ul>
<li>Barbara Harding has scanned her father's wartime diary, and has generously offered it to be included in our daily wartime diary emails. Luckily, advances in AI mean that the handwritten pages can now be converted into text automatically, with good though not complete accuracy.</li>
<li>Can you help add a page from the diary to the daily emails? There will be three steps, that should take you less than an hour in total:
<ol>
<li>Use free AI to extract the text.</li>
<li>Compare the AI-generated text with the original and correct any mistakes.</li>
<li>Post the daily diary entries to the Gwulo website.</li>
</ol>
</li>
<li>If you'd like to help please reply to this email with DIARY in the subject, and I'll send you over a scanned page and instructions on how to process it.</li>
<li>To see the finished results, you can <a href="https://gwulo.com/node/62688"><strong>view the first and last entries from Leo Landau's wartime diary</strong></a>, which I've already transcribed and posted online.<br />&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<li class="ck-list-marker-bold"><strong>Hong Kong &amp; China history talks in the UK</strong>
<ul>
<li>Just two weeks to go until my talks in <a href="https://www.facebook.com/events/3419724694856154/"><strong>York (5 Sep)</strong></a> and <a href="https://www.facebook.com/events/2076682382986169/"><strong>Edinburgh (9 Sep)</strong></a> - I'm looking forward to them!<br />&nbsp;</li>
<li>I'm going to repeat the talks in <strong>Cheltenham</strong> and <strong>Exeter</strong> in October. If you'd like details, please reply with CHELTENHAM or EXTER as your email subject.<br />&nbsp;</li>
<li>Bristol University's <em>Hong Kong History Centre</em> are running their <a href="https://www.hkhistory.net/calendar_event/%e9%a6%99%e6%b8%af%e6%ad%b7%e5%8f%b2%e6%97%a5-hong-kong-history-day-2026/"><strong>Hong Kong History Day on 5 Sep</strong></a>. Two speakers I recognise are Tim Ko and John Carroll from Hong Kong, both of whom give great presentations.<br />&nbsp;</li>
<li>Then later in the month, the Friends of East Asia host host the talk <a href="https://gwulo.com/node/62677"><strong>Chinese Art for Western Interiors on 19 Sep</strong></a> in London. Colin Sheaf, ex Chairman of Bonhams Asia, will give an illustrated talk on the growing enthusiasm for “Chinoiserie” objects in the West during the 17th and 18th centuries.<br />&nbsp;</li>
</ul>
