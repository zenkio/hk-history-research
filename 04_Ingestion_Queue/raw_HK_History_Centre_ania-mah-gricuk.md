Ania Mah Gricuk Hong Kong History Centre Home About News Events Collections Videos People ECR Network Search this site: Search: Home About News Events Collections Videos People ECR Network All Posts --> People Visiting Scholars Ania Mah Gricuk Ania Mah Gricuk is a PhD candidate at the University of California, Santa Cruz. Her research explores medicine, food, and drinks among overseas Chinese communities. Her dissertation, tentatively titled Healing in Diaspora: The Making of Chinese Medicine in Hong Kong, Malay(si)a, and Singapore, 1860s-1980s, examines the co-creation of the category of Chinese Medicine , the development of Chinese charitable hospitals, and the production and consumption of medicine and healing practices in Hong Kong and Southeast Asia. It deconstructs the Chineseness of Chinese medicine as innately tied to China and challenges established understandings of medicine as tied solely to Western biomedicine.
