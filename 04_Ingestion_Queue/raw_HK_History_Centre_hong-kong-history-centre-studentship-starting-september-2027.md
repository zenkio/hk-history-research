Hong Kong History Centre Studentship (starting September 2027) Hong Kong History Centre Home About News Events Collections Videos People ECR Network Search this site: Search: Home About News Events Collections Videos People ECR Network All Posts --> News Opportunities Hong Kong History Centre Studentship (starting September 2027) 24 September 2026 Hong Kong History Centre Studentship The Centre is pleased to announce that it has a studentship opportunity available for a September 2027 start. An award is available to a student embarking on PhD research in any aspect of the History of Hong Kong, broadly conceived, working in the period pre-dating 1950 (with a preference for the pre-war or pre 1930 period). We are looking for applicants interested in pursuing original research to be supervised by members of the Hong Kong History Centre and associated staff in the Department of History (including but not limited to Professor Robert Bickers, Dr Vivian Kong, Dr Peter Hamilton, and Professor Ray Yep), and who also share a vision for public engagement of the discipline. The studentship is funded by a generous donation to the Hong Kong History Centre at the University of Bristol. The Centre encourages and facilitates the study of the history of Hong Kong in the UK, and collaborates with partners in Hong Kong and internationally. Possible doctoral projects could focus on any sub-discipline of history, with a focus on Hong Kong, that could be provided with expert supervision from Hong Kong History Centre staff or other members of the Department of History. It is expected that the project would require Chinese language skills. Alongside their research, the students will help with aspects of public engagement coordinated by members of the Centre. Location: Department of History, School of Humanities, University of Bristol Eligibility: UK and International (including EU) Funding amount: Tuition Fees and stipend of £22,804 per annum (rising in alignment with UKRI), an annual Research Training Support Grant (RTSG) and a generous relocation support if moving from overseas. Dates: Sep 2027 – Sep 2031 (full-time) Candidate requirements: A pass in a Master’s degree (or international equivalent), or be working towards a Master’s degree with a final submission date of no later than 15 September 2026. Knowledge of Chinese language would be desirable. How to apply: It is essential that you complete the following two processes. Apply in writing to hkhistory-centre@bristol.ac.uk , with a formal research proposal, cv, and a statement (no more than 1 page) of your suitability for a role that combines academic research with public engagement. Submit a formal PhD application to the University of Bristol by 27 November 2026 . To make an online application go to http://www.bris.ac.uk/pg-howtoapply . Search and select History PhD on the programme choice page and September 2027 as your start date. Enter details of the scholarship when prompted in the Funding section of the form (Choose ‘Other’ and enter ‘Hong Kong History Centre’ , enter 100 for ‘Percentage from this source’ , and choose ‘Yes’ for funding be secured ). Please also remember to include all documents sent to hkhistory-centre@bristol.ac.uk in your application. Deadline: Applications must be submitted by 5pm BST on Friday 27 November 2026. Please direct any questions relating to the application process to the University of Bristol’s PG Arts Admissions team with ‘School of Humanities’ as part of the subject line: uob-pgradmissions@bristol.ac.uk For Hong Kong History Centre related enquiries, prospective applicants should contact Professor Robert Bickers ( robert.bickers@bristol.ac.uk ), Dr Vivian Kong ( vivian.kong@bristol.ac.uk ), Dr Peter Hamilton ( peter.hamilton@bristol.ac.uk ), or Professor Ray Yep ( rekmy@bristol.ac.uk ).
