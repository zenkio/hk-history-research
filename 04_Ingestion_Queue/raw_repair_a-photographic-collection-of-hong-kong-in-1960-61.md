From the legendary Kai Tak Airport to the vibrant neon lights of Mong Kok and the bustling Star Ferry, we’ll piece together their history using visual clues, old maps, and movie releases. I’ll include scans of all the slides below. Then to see my commentary about the scenes, please click here to watch the video . A big thank you to Chris and Pat Lally, who gave me these slides when we met at my talk in London earlier this year.
