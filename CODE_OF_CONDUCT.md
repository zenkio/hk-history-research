# Code of conduct

This project documents a history that many people lived through and still feel strongly about.

- **Evidence over opinion.** Corrections and additions are welcome when they come with a source.
  Say where a claim comes from; say when sources disagree.
- **Respect people.** Be civil to other contributors and to the people and communities in the history,
  whatever their politics. No harassment, personal attacks or hate speech.
- **Respect copyright and privacy.** Link to and describe rights-held material rather than copying it.
  Do not add private details of living people.

Problems can be raised by opening an issue on this repository. The maintainer may remove content
or contributions that break these rules.
