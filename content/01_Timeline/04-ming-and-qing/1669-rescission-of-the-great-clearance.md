## Background

Following the establishment of the Qing dynasty (大清) in 1644, the Kangxi Emperor's (康熙帝) government faced persistent military resistance from loyalist forces under **Zheng Chenggong** (鄭成功) operating from Taiwan. To cut off supplies and intelligence to these anti-Qing elements, the imperial court issued the Great Clearance (遷界令) starting in **1661**. This drastic policy forced the evacuation of coastal populations several dozen li inland along the Guangdong (廣東) and other southern provinces, devastating the local economy and society of the Pearl River Delta.

## What happened

By the late **1660s**, the military situation had shifted, and the immense human and economic toll of the evacuation became unsustainable. Local officials **Zhou Yide** (周有德) and **Wang Lairen** (王來任) submitted influential memorials detailing the suffering of the displaced people and arguing for the restoration of agricultural production. Recognizing the wisdom of these petitions, the Qing court formally lifted the evacuation order in **1669** (康熙八年). Displaced villagers were permitted to return to their coastal homes, though many found their villages completely ruined, overgrown, and stripped of resources.

## Significance

The rescission of the Great Clearance marked a crucial turning point in the demographic and social history of the region that would become Hong Kong. The returning population was sparse, leading the government to encourage migration from neighboring provinces such as Fujian (福建), Jiangxi (江西), and inland Guangdong to rebuild the agrarian economy. This influx laid the foundational lineage settlement patterns of the New Territories, as various clans re-established their ancestral halls and reclaimed abandoned lands.

## Perspectives

- **Qing Imperial Perspective**: From the perspective of Beijing, the clearance was a necessary strategic military measure to suppress coastal insurgency, and its rescission in 1669 reflected a pragmatic shift toward restoring civil order, taxation, and agricultural stability once the security threat subsided.
- **Local New Territories Communities**: For indigenous lineage communities, the rescission represents a narrative of resilience and survival. Although returning villagers faced the daunting task of reclaiming devastated land from nature, the event enabled the reconstruction of ancestral ties and the eventual resurgence of powerful local clans.
