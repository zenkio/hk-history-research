In response to the incident, the government implemented tighter security measures, increased surveillance on suspected revolutionaries, and reinforced police powers to maintain public order. The event remains a notable example of how the turbulent politics of early 20th-century China directly spilled over into the British-administered territory, forcing the colonial state to reassess its security apparatus.
