## Background

In the aftermath of the Second World War, Hong Kong experienced an unprecedented influx of refugees fleeing the Chinese Civil War and the establishment of the People's Republic of China in **1949**. This massive population surge strained existing infrastructure, leading to severe overcrowding, inadequate sanitation, and the rapid spread of infectious diseases such as tuberculosis. Medical resources were severely overstretched, leaving impoverished communities in makeshift settlements particularly vulnerable.
