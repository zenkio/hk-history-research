## Background

In the aftermath of the 1997 handover and the 1997 Asian financial crisis, the government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (香港特別行政區) sought to diversify its economy away from traditional pillars like manufacturing, real estate, and finance. Influenced by international trends recognizing creative industries as drivers of economic growth, local industry leaders and trade organizations lobbied for institutional support to elevate design standards.

## What happened

On **April 18, 2002**, the **Hong Kong Design Centre (香港設計中心)** was officially launched as a non-profit organization. It was founded by several major local design associations with initial funding and backing from the HKSAR government. The institution was tasked with fostering a design-conscious culture, nurturing creative talent, and connecting businesses with professional designers to enhance industrial competitiveness.

## Significance

The launch is widely viewed as a pivotal moment in the government's recognition of the creative sector as a key economic driver. It laid the institutional groundwork for subsequent initiatives, such as the later development of the **InnoCentre (創新中心)** in **Kowloon Tong (九龍塘)** and the promotion of **Hong Kong (香港)** as a regional design hub.

## Uncertainties

While the official narrative highlights the center as a grassroots-industry collaboration championed by the state, the precise degree of initial industry consensus regarding its governance structure remains subject to differing accounts among local design professionals.

## Perspectives

- **HKSAR Government**: Viewed the center as a vital strategic investment to upgrade the economy's value chain, foster innovation, and transform Hong Kong into a premier regional hub for creative industries.
- **Local Design Community**: Generally welcomed the institutional recognition and resources, though some practitioners debated whether government-backed initiatives would adequately serve independent designers or primarily benefit larger commercial entities.
