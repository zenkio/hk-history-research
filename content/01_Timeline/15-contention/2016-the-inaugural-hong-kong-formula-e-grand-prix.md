Drivers competed in the first round of the 2016–2017 season, with **Sebastien Buemi** emerging as the winner of the inaugural race. Despite the logistical complexity of organizing a street circuit in a densely populated urban center, the event drew significant crowds and international media attention. The race utilized the city's infrastructure to showcase the capabilities of electric mobility, with organizers emphasizing the event's minimal carbon footprint compared to traditional combustion-engine racing.
