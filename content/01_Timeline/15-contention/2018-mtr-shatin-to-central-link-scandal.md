## Background

Initiated to further expand the territory's rapid transit network, the Shatin to Central Link (沙田至中環線) was planned as a major railway project managed by the **MTR Corporation** (香港鐵路有限公司). The multi-billion dollar initiative aimed to connect existing lines to improve cross-harbour transport and alleviate congestion. Throughout the construction phase leading up to **2018**, the project faced various logistical hurdles, but public concern escalated dramatically following investigative media reports.

## What happened

On **May 30, 2018**, media reports exposed major construction irregularities at the Hung Hom station (紅磡站) extension. Whistleblowers and subcontractors alleged that steel reinforcing bars (rebars) on the platform slabs had been intentionally cut short to conceal improper installation, and that safety documentation had been systematically falsified or went missing. Subsequent investigations revealed further unauthorized design changes and structural flaws at other stations along the line, notably at To Kwa Wan station (土瓜灣站), prompting widespread public alarm and government intervention.

## Significance

The scandal severely damaged the reputation of the **MTR Corporation** for engineering excellence and punctuality. It also raised serious questions regarding the effectiveness of government oversight by the **Highways Department** (路政署). The controversy led to legislative inquiries, internal restructuring, and the resignation of several top MTR executives, marking a watershed moment for public scrutiny of major infrastructure projects in Hong Kong.

## Perspectives

- **Local Hong Kong Public and Legislators**: Viewed the scandal as a systemic failure of corporate accountability and government supervision, expressing deep frustration over potential safety hazards and the erosion of transparency in public works.
- **MTR Corporation and Government Officials**: Emphasized that safety remained the top priority while acknowledging serious shortcomings in documentation and contractor management, subsequently pledging comprehensive remediation and strengthened oversight mechanisms.
