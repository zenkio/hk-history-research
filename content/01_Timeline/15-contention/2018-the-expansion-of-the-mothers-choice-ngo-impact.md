## Background
Founded in the late 1980s, **Mother's Choice** (母親的抉擇) has long served as a private voluntary organization dedicated to supporting single girls facing crisis pregnancies and children in need of permanent homes. By **2018**, the organization entered a phase of heightened public visibility and operational expansion. This period occurred against a backdrop of increasing political contention in Hong Kong, where civil society groups faced both a vibrant public interest in social welfare and complex regulatory scrutiny.

## What happened
Throughout **2018**, **Mother's Choice** intensified its advocacy for 'child-centered' care, focusing on the recruitment of foster families and the destigmatization of teenage pregnancy. The organization emphasized its 'every child in a loving family' mission, expanding its collaboration with public hospitals and social service networks. While the organization had historically maintained a low profile, the **2018** period saw more proactive engagement with digital media to influence public discourse. This era saw a broader societal push toward professionalizing the support systems for vulnerable youth, moving away from more traditional, informal community-based support structures that had been prevalent in earlier decades.

## Significance
The expanded presence of **Mother's Choice** in **2018** highlights a shift in Hong Kong’s social welfare discourse. As the city grappled with political polarization, the focus on non-political social issues provided a space for collaboration between private charitable organizations and public institutions. The increased emphasis on adoption and support for teenage mothers signaled a gradual, though debated, evolution in traditional Confucian-influenced attitudes toward family structures. While some stakeholders lauded these developments as essential modernizations of Hong Kong's safety net, others viewed the expansion of private NGOs as an indicator of the state’s changing role in providing comprehensive social welfare.

## Perspectives

- **Social Welfare Advocates**: Advocates argue that the professionalization and expansion of NGOs like Mother's Choice are necessary to fill gaps left by the government, ensuring that marginalized populations receive specialized care. They maintain that destigmatizing pregnancy and encouraging adoption are critical modern human rights goals for the city.
- **Traditionalist Perspectives**: Some community members have historically raised concerns that such organizations might inadvertently challenge traditional, close-knit family structures or influence cultural norms surrounding marriage and child-rearing. These groups often prioritize keeping children within extended family networks rather than relying on institutionalized adoption processes.
