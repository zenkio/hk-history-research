## Background

The enactment of the Law of the People's Republic of China on Safeguarding National Security in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region in June 2020 fundamentally altered the legal landscape of the city. Among its provisions, the law criminalized acts of secession, subversion, terrorism, and collusion with foreign or external forces. **Jimmy Lai** (黎智英), the prominent businessman and founder of the pro-democracy tabloid **Apple Daily** (蘋果日報), had long been a vocal critic of the Beijing and Hong Kong governments. His newspaper and media empire had served as a primary platform for anti-government sentiment and pro-democracy advocacy, particularly during the widespread protests of 2019.

## What happened

On **August 10, 2020**, more than 200 police officers from the National Security Department entered the Tseung Kwan O headquarters of **Apple Daily**. Officers cordoned off the newsroom, examined journalistic materials, and searched the desks of editors and reporters. Concurrently, **Jimmy Lai** was arrested at his home on suspicion of colluding with foreign forces and conspiracy to defraud. Several other executives of **Next Digital** (壹傳媒), including Lai's sons and senior company officials, were also detained in coordinated police operations across the city. The raid sparked immediate condemnation from local journalists' associations and international governments, who expressed deep concern over the protection of journalistic materials and source confidentiality.

## Significance

The raid and subsequent arrests were widely interpreted as a watershed moment for press freedom in Hong Kong. For proponents of the National Security Law, the enforcement action was viewed as a necessary and lawful step to curb activities that endangered national security and allegedly exploited media organizations for foreign collusion. Conversely, critics, press freedom advocates, and various Western governments argued that the operation signaled the erosion of the 'One Country, Two Systems' framework and the silencing of independent media. The events of **August 10, 2020**, set a precedent that ultimately led to the closure of **Apple Daily** in June 2021 following further asset freezes and arrests.

## Perspectives

- **Hong Kong and Beijing Authorities**: The actions taken against Jimmy Lai and Apple Daily were lawful enforcement measures under the National Security Law to address suspected criminal conspiracies and foreign collusion, emphasizing that freedom of the press is not absolute and does not protect illegal acts.
- **Local Pro-Democracy and International Observers**: The raid on the newsroom and the arrest of a prominent publisher represented a severe blow to freedom of speech and press independence, effectively dismantling the open media environment that had long characterized Hong Kong.
