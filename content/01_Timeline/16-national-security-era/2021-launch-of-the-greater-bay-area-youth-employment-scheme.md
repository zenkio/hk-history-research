## Background

Following the implementation of the National Security Law in mid-2020 and amidst high local youth unemployment exacerbated by economic shifts and the COVID-19 pandemic, the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region government sought deeper integration with the mainland Chinese economy. The Greater Bay Area (大灣區) initiative, linking Hong Kong and Macao with nine cities in Guangdong Province (廣東省), had been a long-standing policy priority aimed at diversifying Hong Kong's economy and leveraging mainland technological growth.

## What happened

On **January 8, 2021**, the HKSAR government officially launched the **Greater Bay Area Youth Employment Scheme** (大灣區青年就業計劃). The initiative offered financial incentives to enterprises with operations in both Hong Kong and mainland Greater Bay Area cities to hire recent Hong Kong university graduates. Under the scheme, the government subsidized participating companies to pay participating graduates a monthly allowance of up to HK$18,000 for a duration of up to 18 months. The scheme specifically targeted technology-driven sectors and modern enterprises, aiming to place hundreds, and later thousands, of young professionals across the border to gain work experience in mainland tech hubs such as Shenzhen (深圳).

## Significance

The scheme marked a concrete step in Beijing and Hong Kong's broader strategy to intertwine the city's professional workforce with the mainland's innovation ecosystem. While proponents viewed it as essential career diversification for local youth facing a saturated domestic market, critics and independent observers analyzed it as part of a systematic effort to accelerate political and economic assimilation into the People's Republic of China, potentially shifting the professional orientation of the next generation of Hongkongers northward rather than internationally.

## Perspectives

- **HKSAR Government and Beijing**: The scheme provides valuable career opportunities and broad horizons for Hong Kong youth, enabling them to capture the immense growth potential of the Greater Bay Area and contribute to national development.
- **Local critics and overseas observers**: The initiative is viewed as a top-down policy tool designed to accelerate the integration of Hong Kong's professional class into mainland China, potentially diminishing the city's distinct international character.
