## Background

Following the landslide victory of the pro-democracy camp in the **November 2019** District Council elections, opposition politicians gained control of 17 out of 18 district councils across Hong Kong. These grassroots bodies, historically consultative, became the last remaining representative public institutions with a democratic mandate following the enactment of the **National Security Law** in **June 2020** and subsequent electoral overhauls.

## What happened

In **July 2021**, mounting pressure from pro-establishment media and official sources suggested that district councillors would be required to take an oath of allegiance under the Public Offices (Candidacy and Taking Up Offices) (Miscellaneous Amendments) Ordinance 2021. Rumors circulated that those disqualified for failing the oath would face bankruptcy-inducing retrospective fines, covering all salaries, operational expenses, and staff costs drawn since taking office. Consequently, between **July 8 and July 22, 2021**, more than 200 pro-democracy councillors tendered their resignations, effectively paralyzing the functioning of multiple district councils.

## Significance

The mass resignations marked the virtual elimination of the organized opposition from institutional politics in Hong Kong. Without elected local representatives, the governance of districts such as Sha Tin (沙田) and Central and Western (中西區) shifted entirely to government-appointed committees and civil servants, completing a major structural transition in the territory's political landscape.

## Perspectives

- **Beijing and Hong Kong Government**: Authorities maintained that the oath-taking requirement was a legal necessity to ensure that public office holders uphold the Basic Law and swear allegiance to the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region, viewing the departures as individual choices rather than forced removals.
- **Pro-Democracy Camp and Supporters**: Pro-democracy figures argued that the retrospective financial threats and vague disqualification criteria constituted a deliberate political campaign to purge opposition voices from all levels of governance.
