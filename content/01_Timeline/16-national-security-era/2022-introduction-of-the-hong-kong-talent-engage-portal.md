## Background
Following the implementation of the **National Security Law** in 2020 and subsequent social and political shifts, Hong Kong experienced a notable period of emigration. By late 2022, official figures and business associations reported a contraction in the local labor force, particularly affecting high-skilled sectors such as finance, information technology, and healthcare. The government identified the retention and acquisition of global talent as a strategic priority to maintain Hong Kong's status as an international financial hub and to address the demographic challenges posed by the aging population and the departure of skilled residents.

## What happened
On **December 28, 2022**, the HKSAR government officially launched the **Hong Kong Talent Engage** (人才服務窗口) online portal. Managed by the **Talent Service Unit** (人才服務窗口辦公室), the website serves as a one-stop digital interface for international professionals and mainland Chinese talent. The platform provides consolidated information regarding various immigration schemes, such as the **Top Talent Pass Scheme** (高端人才通行證計劃) and the **General Employment Policy** (一般就業政策). The portal was designed to streamline the application process, provide information on living in Hong Kong, and connect incoming professionals with local employment opportunities. The launch was accompanied by a government-led promotion campaign to market Hong Kong’s business environment to overseas markets.

## Significance
The introduction of the portal represented a formal shift in Hong Kong’s migration policy toward aggressive recruitment. Proponents within the administration viewed the initiative as a proactive recovery measure to restore economic competitiveness and stabilize the labor market. Conversely, the necessity of such a platform has been debated by various stakeholders. Some commentators have interpreted the need for aggressive recruitment as a direct admission of the severity of the human capital flight, while others have viewed it as a natural evolution of global talent competition. The long-term efficacy of the portal in reversing the net outflow of professionals remains a subject of ongoing observation, as the influx of new arrivals is evaluated against the backdrop of changing geopolitical dynamics and the integration of Hong Kong into the regional development framework of the **Greater Bay Area** (粵港澳大灣區).

## Perspectives

- **HKSAR Government**: The administration views the portal as a vital tool for injecting new momentum into the economy. They emphasize that attracting top-tier global talent is essential for maintaining Hong Kong's institutional competitiveness and long-term prosperity.
- **Pro-democracy commentators and overseas observers**: Some observers argue that the portal is a state-led response to talent losses caused by political instability and the erosion of perceived civil liberties. They suggest that the government must address the underlying causes of emigration to ensure the sustainable retention of talent.
