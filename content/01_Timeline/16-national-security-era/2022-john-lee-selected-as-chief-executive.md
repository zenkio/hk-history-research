## Background

Following the widespread political unrest of **2019**, the central government in Beijing and the Hong Kong government moved to reshape the territory's political landscape. In **2020**, Beijing enacted the Law of the People's Republic of China on Safeguarding National Security in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (National Security Law). This was followed in **2021** by a comprehensive overhaul of Hong Kong’s electoral system, designed to implement the principle of "patriots administering Hong Kong" (愛國者治港). The Election Committee, responsible for choosing the Chief Executive, was expanded and restructured to ensure tighter alignment with Beijing's governance requirements.

## What happened

On **May 8, 2022**, **John Lee** (李家超), who had recently resigned from his position as Chief Secretary for Administration and formerly served as Secretary for Security, stood as the sole candidate in the Chief Executive election. Out of the 1,464 members of the Election Committee who cast votes, **Lee** received 1,416 support votes, securing his victory with approximately 99.2% of the valid ballots cast. His candidacy and subsequent selection marked the first time a Chief Executive candidate ran without a concurrent challenger since the handover of sovereignty in **1997**.

## Significance

**Lee**’s selection signified a notable shift in Hong Kong's administrative focus toward institutional security and law enforcement, given his extensive background in the police force and security apparatus. Proponents viewed the election as a crucial step toward restoring social stability and executive-led governance following years of political turmoil. Conversely, critics and overseas observers raised concerns regarding the lack of competitive candidates and the continued erosion of democratic representation under the modified electoral framework.

## Perspectives

- **Beijing and the Hong Kong Government**: The election was viewed as a successful implementation of the improved electoral system, ensuring that only staunch patriots hold political office to maintain long-term stability and prosperity.
- **Critics and International Observers**: The uncontested nature of the election and the heavy security-oriented background of the sole candidate were seen as further evidence of Beijing tightening its control and reducing political pluralism in Hong Kong.
