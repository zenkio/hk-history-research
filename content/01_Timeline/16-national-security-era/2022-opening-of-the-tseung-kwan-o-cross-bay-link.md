## Background

The development of Tseung Kwan O (將軍澳) as a new town since the 1980s led to rapid population growth and significant traffic congestion during peak commuting hours. To alleviate pressure on existing land-based routes connecting the area to Kowloon and other parts of the territory, the government planned various infrastructure enhancements, including the Tseung Kwan O-Lam Tin Tunnel (將軍澳藍田隧道) and the Cross Bay Link (將軍澳跨灣連接路).

## What happened

On **December 11, 2022**, the Tseung Kwan O Cross Bay Link officially opened to traffic, alongside the concurrent opening of the Tseung Kwan O-Lam Tin Tunnel. The project's centerpiece is the **Infinity Bridge** (慶澳橋/or formally referred to locally as the cross-bay marine viaduct), an iconic 1.8-kilometer steel arch bridge. Notably, it became Hong Kong's first marine viaduct to incorporate a dedicated cycle track and footpath alongside vehicular lanes, offering both functional transportation and recreational space.

## Significance

The opening significantly transformed local transit by providing a more direct route between Tseung Kwan O and the eastern districts of Kowloon, reducing travel times for daily commuters. From a urban planning perspective, the integration of active transport facilities—specifically the pedestrian walkway and cycling track connected to the existing cycling network—marked a departure from purely vehicle-centric infrastructure projects in the city, though opinions on its long-term recreational impact vary among residents and urban planners.

## Perspectives

- **Government and Transport Planners**: Authorities emphasize that the Cross Bay Link successfully relieves traffic congestion in the new town while promoting green mobility through integrated pedestrian and cycling facilities.
- **Local Residents and Cyclists**: Many community members welcomed the new recreational amenities and scenic seaside pathways, while some raised concerns regarding weekend crowding and the adequacy of connecting bike lanes in older parts of the district.
