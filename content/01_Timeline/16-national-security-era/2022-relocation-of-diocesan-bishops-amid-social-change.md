## Background

Historically, the Vatican has maintained a complex diplomatic balancing act, seeking to protect the interests of Catholics in mainland China while navigating the political realities of Hong Kong. This delicate position has frequently influenced the public pronouncements and administrative decisions of the local hierarchy.

## What happened

The period surrounding late 2022 was marked by internal administrative adjustments as the diocese navigated the new legal environment. While the Church remains a major provider of education and social services, its role as a vocal critic or advocate on sensitive political matters has been largely tempered. The events of late 2022 are viewed by many as a clear indicator of the diocese's attempt to preserve its functional existence while adhering to the mandates imposed by the local government and the requirements of the Sino-Vatican provisional agreement.
