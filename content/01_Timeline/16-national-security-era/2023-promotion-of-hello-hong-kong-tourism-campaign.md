## Background
By early **2023**, the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region (HKSAR) had begun lifting the stringent social distancing and mandatory quarantine policies that had defined the city’s response to the COVID-19 pandemic. These measures had significantly curtailed international travel and business activity, leading to a period of relative isolation. During this time, the city also underwent major political changes following the implementation of the **National Security Law** in **2020**, which altered the landscape of public discourse and governance. As global borders reopened, the government sought to signal that Hong Kong was 'back on the map' and open for business and tourism.

## What happened
On **February 2, 2023**, the HKSAR government, led by Chief Executive **John Lee** (李家超), officially inaugurated the 'Hello Hong Kong' (你好，香港！) campaign. Organized primarily by the **Hong Kong Tourism Board** (香港旅遊發展局), the campaign was designed to reach a global audience. A core component of the initiative involved the distribution of approximately 500,000 free flight tickets, a program supported by the **Airport Authority Hong Kong** (香港機場管理局) to encourage visitors from international markets and the Chinese Mainland to return to the city. The promotional materials featured celebrities and influencers tasked with highlighting the city’s culinary, cultural, and urban attractions. The government positioned the campaign as a major milestone in Hong Kong's post-pandemic recovery, emphasizing the city's unique position as a gateway between East and West.

## Significance
The launch of 'Hello Hong Kong' served as a symbolic turning point, moving the territory away from the 'Zero-COVID' policy framework. For the government, the campaign was a necessary economic intervention to revive the tourism and retail sectors, which had suffered from years of depressed activity. However, the campaign also functioned as a soft power projection intended to counter critical international narratives that had emerged regarding Hong Kong’s political stability. Whether the campaign fully succeeded in rehabilitating the city's global reputation remains a subject of ongoing debate, as observers weigh the success of economic indicators against the broader international concerns regarding the shifting legal and political environment of the territory.

## Perspectives

- **Government and Pro-establishment perspective**: The administration viewed the campaign as a essential economic stimulus necessary to restore Hong Kong's role as an international financial and tourism hub. Supporters argued that the campaign successfully demonstrated that the city remained a vibrant, safe, and accessible destination for global travelers.
- **International and Civil Society perspective**: Some international analysts and civil society groups questioned whether promotional campaigns could offset the perceived impact of the National Security Law on the city's image. These observers often argued that potential visitors and business investors remained hesitant due to concerns about the evolving rule of law and the restricted political environment.
