## Background
Under Article 23 of the Hong Kong Basic Law (香港基本法), the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region is required to enact its own laws to prohibit acts of treason, secession, sedition, and subversion against the Central People's Government. A previous attempt to pass this legislation in 2003 led to massive public protests, resulting in the government indefinitely postponing the bill. Following the implementation of the Beijing-imposed National Security Law in 2020, pressure mounted to complete the local legislative process.

## What happened
On **March 8, 2024**, the Safeguarding National Security Bill (維護國家安全條例草案) was formally introduced to the Legislative Council (立法會). The legislative process moved rapidly, with the bill receiving unanimous approval from lawmakers on **March 19, 2024**. Chief Executive **John Lee** (李家超) gazetted the law, and it officially came into effect on **March 23, 2024**, creating new offenses related to treason, insurrection, espionage, sabotage, and external interference, while broadening the legal definitions surrounding seditious intentions and the handling of state secrets.

## Significance
The enactment marked the fulfillment of Hong Kong's long-standing constitutional obligation under the Basic Law, closing what authorities viewed as a critical loophole in national security. However, the legislation drew intense international scrutiny regarding its potential impact on civil liberties, human rights, and freedom of expression in the territory.

## Perspectives

- **Beijing and Hong Kong Governments**: Authorities maintain that the legislation is vital for plugging national security loopholes, ensuring long-term stability, and protecting the city from foreign interference, while aligning with international legal standards.
- **Critics and Overseas Governments**: International observers, foreign governments, and rights advocates argue that the law's broad definitions of state secrets, espionage, and sedition further erode civil liberties and align Hong Kong's legal system more closely with mainland China.
