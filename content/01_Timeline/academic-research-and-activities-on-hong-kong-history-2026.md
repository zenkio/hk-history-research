The Hong Kong History Centre at Bristol hosted a diverse range of scholarly engagements from **April to June 2026**. **Dr. Shuk-wah Poon** presented on the socio-political history of pre-war sea bathing, while **Ka Shing So** examined the illicit gold trade during the Cold War. **Dr. Lok-yin Law** analyzed the commemorative politics surrounding the Red House, and **Dr. Doris Chan** researched post-war migration policies and decolonization. Additionally, **Dr. Nadine Attewell** discussed historical racial intimacy in colonial port cities, and **Tracy Leung** explored the political role of youth organizations in Hong Kong. These activities were supplemented by the launch of a new academic Summer School and a comprehensive lecture series.
