**Robert John Vaughan Grindley**, a former Royal Navy sailor turned prison officer, served in the **Stanley Platoon** of the **Hong Kong Volunteer Defence Corps** during the **1941** Battle of Hong Kong. Following the colony's surrender, he and his wife, **Marjorie Betsy Grindley**, a volunteer nurse, were interned at the **Stanley Civilian Internment Camp** from **1942** to **1945**. According to a blog post by a local historian, the couple later retired to the Isle of Sheppey. Medals belonging to **Robert Grindley** were rediscovered in **2018** and transferred to the **Eastchurch Aviation Museum**. The source highlights the rarity of his **Hong Kong Efficiency Medal** and details the couple's lives through military records and **Marjorie Grindley**'s oral history interviews.
