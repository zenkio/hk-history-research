**Brigadier Cedric Wallis** served as an infantry brigade commander during the **1941** Battle for Hong Kong. Following the British capitulation on **1941-12-25**, he endured incarceration in Japanese prison camps. According to the source, **Wallis** was a controversial figure who clashed with Canadian leadership, including **Lt-Col Home**, over military operations. After the war, he left the British Indian Army and eventually settled in Canada in **1953**. The discovery of a trunk belonging to him in Western Australia highlights his post-war travels and personal history.
