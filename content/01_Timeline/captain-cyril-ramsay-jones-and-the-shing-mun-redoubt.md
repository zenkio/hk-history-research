Captain **Cyril Ramsay Jones** of the **2nd Battalion Royal Scots** commanded 'A' Company during the defense of Hong Kong. On **9 December 1941**, the Shing Mun Redoubt on the Gin Drinkers Line fell to Japanese forces while **Jones** was stationed in the Artillery Observation Post. According to the source, brigade commander **Brigadier Cedric Wallis** viewed **Jones** as a weak company commander prior to the outbreak of hostilities. **Jones** faced significant post-war scrutiny regarding his leadership and accountability for the loss of the defensive position. The blog post examines his background, pre-war reputation in Hong Kong, and the circumstances surrounding the redoubt's capture.
