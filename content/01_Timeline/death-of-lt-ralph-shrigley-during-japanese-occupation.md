**Ralph James Shrigley**, an officer in the **Hong Kong Volunteer Defence Corps** (HKVDC), was captured and interned by Japanese forces following the **1941** surrender of Hong Kong. According to research by a military history blog, **Shrigley** was removed from the Shamshuipo POW camp in June **1944** for interrogation at Victoria Gaol. Testimony from a post-war war crimes trial provided by **Lai Chung-yiu** detailed how **Shrigley** was subjected to severe torture, including waterboarding and beatings, by a Japanese gendarme named **Ushigai**. Unable to endure the abuse, **Shrigley** died by suicide on **June 28, 1944**, after jumping from the third floor of his cell block. While rumors suggested his death was related to the location of buried regimental colours, the source suggests it was more likely due to Japanese efforts to extract intelligence regarding resistance activities.
