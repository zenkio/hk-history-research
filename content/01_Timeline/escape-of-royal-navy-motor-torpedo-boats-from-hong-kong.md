Following the British surrender of Hong Kong on **December 25, 1941**, Commodore **Alfred Collinson** ordered the remaining Royal Navy Motor Torpedo Boats (MTBs) to escape. Lt-Cdr **Gerard Horace Gandy** oversaw the mission, which included extracting the Chinese Admiral **Chan Chak** and his staff to Free China. According to the source, **Gandy** and SOE operative **Francis Woodley 'Mike' Kendall** decided to wait for nightfall to avoid Japanese detection, despite initial orders to depart in daylight. This daring operation allowed several key military figures to evade capture after the fall of the colony.
