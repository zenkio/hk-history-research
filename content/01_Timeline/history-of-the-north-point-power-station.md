The **Hong Kong Electric Company** established the **North Point** power station to replace outdated infrastructure and meet rising energy demands. According to the Industrial History of Hong Kong Group, the site began generating electricity in **1919** using equipment sourced from the Yukon Territory. By **1932**, the facility implemented advanced **Brush-Ljungstrom** steam-turbine generators to improve efficiency. Following the **1922** Seamen's Strike, the company prioritized securing its own wharf, eventually completing **Mackie's Wharf** in **1935** to streamline coal logistics. This facility significantly modernized electricity production on the island before urban development eventually replaced the industrial site.
