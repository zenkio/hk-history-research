The **North Point power station** was established to provide modern electricity to Hong Kong using steam turbine technology. According to **Hugh Farmer** and the **Industrial History of Hong Kong Group**, the station began operations in **1919** using equipment sourced from **Dawson City, Canada**. Throughout the **1920s**, the facility underwent significant expansion, reaching a capacity of 28,000 kilowatts within a decade. The site famously constructed **Mackie’s Wharf** in **1935** to streamline coal handling, marking a shift away from manual junk-based logistics. The infrastructure development was influenced by the **1922** Seamen’s Strike, which highlighted the vulnerability of the company's reliance on manual labor.
