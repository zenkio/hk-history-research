On **24 September 2026**, the **Hong Kong History Centre** at the **University of Bristol** announced a PhD studentship opportunity for the academic year commencing in **September 2027**. The program seeks a doctoral candidate to conduct original research on any aspect of Hong Kong history, specifically focusing on the period prior to **1950**, with a stated preference for projects covering the pre-war era or the period before **1930**. This research initiative is intended to be supervised by faculty members of the **University of Bristol’s Department of History**, including **Professor Robert Bickers**, **Dr. Vivian Kong**, **Dr. Peter Hamilton**, and **Professor Ray Yep**.

The studentship is supported by a donation to the **Hong Kong History Centre** and includes funding for tuition fees, an annual stipend of **£22,804** (adjusted for **UKRI** alignment), and a research training support grant. Applicants must demonstrate a commitment to both academic research and public engagement within the discipline, and it is expected that the proposed projects will require proficiency in the **Chinese** language. Candidates were required to possess a Master's degree or be on track to complete one by **15 September 2026**. The application process, which concluded on **27 November 2026**, mandated both a direct submission of a research proposal to the Centre and a formal doctoral application to the university.
