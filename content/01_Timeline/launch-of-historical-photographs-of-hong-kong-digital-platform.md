On **9 September 2026**, the **Hong Kong History Centre** at the **University of Bristol** launched an open-access digital platform titled "Historical Photographs of Hong Kong." This resource provides public access to a digitized collection of slides, prints, and negatives held within the **University of Bristol Library**. According to the **Hong Kong History Centre**, the project serves to preserve and share archival visual records of Hong Kong's past. The platform was accompanied by a blog post detailing the collection's development and historical significance.
