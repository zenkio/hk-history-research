During the **Battle for Hong Kong** in **1941**, **Major Arthur Dewar** of the **Royal Army Service Corps (RASC)** managed the Army's maritime transport operations. According to the source, **Dewar** oversaw a fleet of vessels, including lighters and launches, which facilitated the evacuation of stores and personnel, including mules, from the mainland to Hong Kong Island during the **WM Plan** withdrawal. **Dewar** notably commanded the unit that held the Shouson Hill ordnance magazines, marking the final organized resistance prior to the British surrender. For his actions, he was awarded the **Distinguished Service Order (DSO)** in **1946** for conspicuous gallantry.
