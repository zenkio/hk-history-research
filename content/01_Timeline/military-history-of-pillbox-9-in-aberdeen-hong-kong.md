During the **1941** Battle of Hong Kong, Pillbox 9 served as a Beach Defence Unit on Aberdeen Praya Road, commanded by **Sgt Thomas Richard Montague Castle** and manned by 'A' Company of the **1/Mx**. According to the author **Philip Cracknell** and the **UK National Archives**, the unit provided defensive support for the Aberdeen naval base until Japanese forces seized the island. Following the fall of the colony, **Sgt Castle** became a prisoner of war and eventually perished after the sinking of the **Lisbon Maru** in **1942**. The pillbox structure, which featured a companion Lyon Light, was eventually demolished post-war. The deployment history of these units was documented in the **1/Mx War Diary** (WO 172/1689).
