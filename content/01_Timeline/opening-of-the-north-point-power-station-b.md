The **Hongkong Electric Company** officially inaugurated its new **North Point Power Station B** on **December 15, 1958**. According to the *China Mail*, the facility featured an initial power unit of 30,000 kilowatts, with designs allowing for the future installation of four additional units. Construction of the plant, which cost an estimated $25 million, commenced in **August 1955**. The **English Electric Co** managed the generation plant assembly, while **Babcock Wilcox Ltd**, **British Insulated Callender’s Cables**, and **George Wimpey Co Ltd** served as major sub-contractors for the project.
