In **1950**, the colonial administration initiated a project to elevate the level of **Queen's Road Central** between **Ice House Street** and **Pedder Street**. According to **The China Mail** on **October 31, 1950**, construction was scheduled to begin in the second half of **December 1950**. The project necessitated the closure of the specified stretch of road to all vehicular traffic starting from **November 6, 1950**. The authorities estimated that the work would conclude by **February 1951**, resulting in a total closure period of approximately four months.
