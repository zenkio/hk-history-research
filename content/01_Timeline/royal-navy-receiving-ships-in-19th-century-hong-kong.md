Throughout the 19th century, the British Royal Navy utilized 'receiving ships' in Victoria Harbour to serve as hospitals, storehouses, and headquarters. According to Philip Cracknell, vessels such as **HMS Minden**, commissioned in **1842**, and **HMS Alligator** provided essential infrastructure for the colonial naval presence. Other notable ships like **HMS Victor Emanuel** and **HMS Tamar** transitioned from active service to hulked status as the fleet modernized from sail to steam power. The service of these vessels concluded in **1941** when **HMS Tamar** was scuttled during the Japanese invasion of Hong Kong.
