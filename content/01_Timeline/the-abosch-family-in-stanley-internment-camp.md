During the Japanese occupation of Hong Kong, **Josef Abosch**, his wife **Rosa**, and their son **Ralph Frank** were interned at the Stanley Internment Camp. According to research by **Philip Cracknell**, the family was assigned to the Indian Quarters in **1941/1945**. **Rosa Abosch** and her son were sheltered at the Matilda Hospital on **1941-12-23** before entering the camp. Following their liberation in **1945**, the family traveled to the United Kingdom, after which **Josef Abosch** migrated to the United States and eventually passed away in **1976**. The source notes that the subsequent fates of **Rosa** and **Ralph** remain a subject of historical inquiry.
