On **15 February 1942**, **Lt General Arthur Percival** surrendered British, Australian, Indian, and Malay troops to **Lt General Tomoyuki Yamashita** at the former **Ford Motor Factory** in Singapore. As reported in the source text, **Winston Churchill** characterized this event as the largest capitulation in British history. The Japanese victory followed a rapid campaign through Malaya, exploiting British deficiencies in aircraft, armor, and air-sea superiority. Historians cited in the article debate whether the defeat resulted from **Percival's** lack of leadership or an insurmountable disadvantage in resources and intelligence. The capture of the city resulted in thousands of Allied personnel being interned in harsh conditions as prisoners of war.
