In **February 1942**, amidst the imminent fall of Singapore, several high-ranking British military officers attempted to evacuate the island to Batavia. According to the blog post by the author of 'Battle for Hong Kong', **Rear Admiral Ernest Spooner**, **Air Vice Marshal Conway Pulford**, and **Commander Pendarvis Lister Frampton** departed on **13 February 1942** aboard the Fairmile-class motor launch **ML 310**. Commanded by **Lt Jonny Bull**, the vessel sought to escape as Japanese forces established dominance over the island. The source reports that these senior officers never reached their destination, instead perishing later that year on a remote island near Sumatra. The article also chronicles the concurrent escape of **Peggy Frampton**, who successfully evacuated her family to India before the Japanese occupation.
