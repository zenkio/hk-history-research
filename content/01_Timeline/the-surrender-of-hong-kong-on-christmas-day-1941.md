The surrender of British forces in **Hong Kong** was formalized on **December 25, 1941**, following an eighteen-day conflict often termed 'Black Christmas.' According to the source, Major General **Christopher Michael Maltby** dispatched staff officers to Murray Barracks to instruct Lieutenant Colonel **Henry Moncrieff Stewart** to negotiate the capitulation. Prime Minister **Winston Churchill** had previously identified the colony as an untenable strategic liability that could neither be defended nor relieved. Despite the arrival of **C-Force** Canadian reinforcements in **November 1941**, the garrison remained militarily weak and largely isolated. The source notes that the rapid destruction of antiquated RAF assets at Kai Tak during the initial Japanese air raids further undermined the British defensive position.
