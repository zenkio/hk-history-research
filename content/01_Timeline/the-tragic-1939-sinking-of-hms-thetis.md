The submarine **HMS Thetis** sank during sea trials in Liverpool Bay on **1 June 1939**, resulting in ninety-nine fatalities. According to the source, the accident occurred when the inner torpedo tube door was opened while the outer door was also open, causing the forward compartments to flood due to a blocked test cock. The vessel, which carried double its normal complement, remained on the seabed before being salvaged and renamed **HMS Thunderbolt**. The source notes that this event remains the worst peacetime loss of a submarine in British naval history. The submarine was eventually sunk during wartime service in March **1943**.
