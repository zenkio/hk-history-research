During the outbreak of the Pacific War in **December 1941**, Captain **Archibald Cook** was operating the SS Fatshan between Hong Kong and Canton when he was taken into custody by Japanese forces. His wife, **Frances Cook**, and their three youngest children were interned at **Stanley Camp** in Hong Kong, while their three older children remained in northern China. According to the source, **Archibald Cook** was held under house arrest in Canton for four months before being moved to an internment center and eventually repatriated in **May 1942**. The family was briefly reunited in **August 1942** in Lourenco Marques before **Archibald Cook** returned to maritime service in the South Pacific. This account highlights the disruption caused to families on the China Coast during the **1941**-**1942** conflict period.
