During the **Japanese occupation** of Hong Kong, the **MS Gripsholm** repatriated American citizens in **1942**, arriving in New York Harbour on **August 25, 1942**. On **September 8, 1942**, Japanese forces released Chinese, Malay, and Annamite members of the **HKVDC** from the **Sham Shui Po** POW camp. Following the defeat of Japan, the **Allied fleet** arrived in **Victoria Harbour** on **August 30, 1945**. Official surrender ceremonies occurred at **Government House** on **September 16, 1945**, followed by the departure of former internees and prisoners of war for their homes.
