## Early Life and Commercial Foundation

**Alexander Matheson** (1805–1886) was a Scottish merchant who, alongside **William Jardine** (威廉·渣甸), co-founded the influential trading firm **Jardine, Matheson & Co.** (怡和洋行) in **1832**. Operating initially from Canton, the firm became the largest British trading house in the region. Following the outbreak of the First Opium War, **Matheson** was instrumental in identifying the potential of the deep-water harbor at Hong Kong Island, which the firm utilized as a strategic base for their shipping and opium trade operations.

## Influence in Colonial Hong Kong

**Matheson**'s role in the development of Hong Kong remains a subject of historical analysis. Conversely, modern scholarly interpretations often examine his activities through the lens of the colonial exploitation and the negative social impacts of the opium trade, noting the ethical debates surrounding the wealth generated by his firm.

Historical accounts regarding **Matheson** often differ.
