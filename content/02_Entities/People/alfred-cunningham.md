## Career in Hong Kong
**Alfred Cunningham** (康寧) was a prominent British journalist active in Hong Kong during the early 20th century. He is best remembered for his contribution to the local media landscape as one of the founders of the **South China Morning Post** (**南華早報**), which published its inaugural issue on **November 6, 1903**. Alongside **Tse Tsan-tai** (謝纘泰), Cunningham sought to create a newspaper that provided a comprehensive English-language perspective on local and regional affairs.
