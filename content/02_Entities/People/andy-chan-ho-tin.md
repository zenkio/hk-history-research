In **2016**, Chan co-founded the **Hong Kong National Party** (香港民族黨), making it the first political organization in the territory to explicitly advocate for Hong Kong independence and the establishment of a republic. 

## The Ban of the Hong Kong National Party

The activities of **Chan** and his party precipitated a major constitutional and security clash with the governments in Hong Kong and Beijing. In **September 2018**, the Hong Kong government formally banned the **Hong Kong National Party** under the Societies Ordinance, citing national security, public safety, and public order. This marked the first time since the **1997** handover that a political party was formally outlawed.
