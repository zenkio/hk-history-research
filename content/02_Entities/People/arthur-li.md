## Early Career and Academic Leadership
**Arthur Li** (李國章) is a descendant of the prominent **Li** family, known for its long-standing influence in Hong Kong business and public life. An accomplished surgeon, **Li** served as the Vice-Chancellor of the **Chinese University of Hong Kong** (CUHK) from **1996** to **2002**. His tenure was marked by significant restructuring efforts, which drew both support for his drive toward academic modernization and criticism from staff and faculty members concerned about administrative centralization.

## Secretary for Education and Manpower
In **2002**, **Li** was appointed Secretary for Education and Manpower by the HKSAR government. During his time in office, he became a central figure in the implementation of the **New Senior Secondary** (NSS) curriculum. This educational reform, which sought to shift Hong Kong toward a "3+3+4" academic structure, was a subject of intense debate. Proponents argued it was necessary to align Hong Kong with international standards and foster a more holistic education. Critics, including various teacher unions and parents, voiced concerns over the implementation timeline, the increased workload for students, and the perceived top-down management style adopted by the Education and Manpower Bureau under his leadership.
