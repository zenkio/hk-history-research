## Life and Scholarship
**Au Tai-tin** (區大典) was a scholar from Shunde, Guangdong, who attained the rank of *juren* during the late Qing dynasty. Following the collapse of the imperial examination system and the establishment of the Republic of China, he relocated to Hong Kong. He is primarily remembered for his efforts to preserve traditional Confucian values and classical literature amidst the rapid modernization and Westernization occurring under British colonial rule.

## Role in Hong Kong Education
Au was a central figure in the promotion of Chinese studies in the colony. Au served as a professor of Chinese at the University of Hong Kong and was instrumental in the establishment of the **Tang Shau Kin**-funded initiatives. He frequently lectured at the **Confucian Hall** (孔聖會), which became a hub for scholars who viewed traditional Chinese learning as a stabilizing force for the local Chinese community.
