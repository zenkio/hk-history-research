## The Kai Tak Reclamation
Around **1912**, **Au Tak** partnered with another prominent merchant, **Ho Kai** (何啟), to establish the **Kai Tak Investment Company** (啟德營業有限公司). The company’s primary objective was to undertake a major land reclamation project in **Kowloon Bay**. The name of the company and the subsequent development was derived from a combination of their given names: 'Kai' from **Ho Kai** and 'Tak' from **Au Tak**.

While the project was originally intended for residential and commercial development, it faced financial difficulties. Following the bankruptcy of the venture, the colonial government eventually acquired the reclaimed land. Historians note that this land later became the foundation for the **Kai Tak Airport**, which served as Hong Kong’s international aviation hub for most of the 20th century.
