## Early Career and Academic Influence
**Benny Tai Yiu-ting** (戴耀廷) served as an associate professor of law at the **University of Hong Kong** for many years, specializing in constitutional law and administrative law. Throughout the 2000s and early 2010s, he was widely regarded as a respected scholar who contributed to legal education and public discourse regarding the implementation of the **Basic Law** (基本法).

## Advocacy and Political Movements
In **2013**, **Tai** initiated the **Occupy Central with Love and Peace** movement, advocating for universal suffrage in Hong Kong's Chief Executive elections. This movement became a foundational element of the **2014 Hong Kong protests**, commonly known as the **Umbrella Movement**. Proponents of the movement viewed his advocacy as a legitimate exercise of civil rights and a defense of the promise of "dual universal suffrage" contained in the Basic Law. Conversely, the Hong Kong government and the central authorities in **Beijing** characterized his actions as illegal acts that disrupted social order and challenged the constitutional authority of the state.

## Legal Consequences and Judicial Perspectives
In **2019**, **Tai** was convicted of public nuisance-related charges stemming from his role in the **2014** protests and was subsequently dismissed from his tenured position at the **University of Hong Kong**. In **2020**, he was a primary organizer of an unofficial primary election for the pro-democracy camp. This led to his arrest and subsequent prosecution under the **National Security Law** (國家安全法), which was enacted in June **2020**.
