## Academic Career and Early Advocacy

**Benny Tai** (戴耀廷) served as an associate professor in the Faculty of Law at the University of Hong Kong, specializing in constitutional law, administrative law, and the Hong Kong Basic Law. Throughout the 2000s and early 2010s, **Tai** participated in public discussions regarding Hong Kong's constitutional development, consistently advocating for universal suffrage in accordance with the Hong Kong Basic Law.

## The Umbrella Movement

In **2013**, **Tai**, along with **Chan Kin-man** (陳健民) and **Chu Yiu-ming** (朱耀明), co-founded the **Occupy Central with Love and Peace** (讓愛與和平佔領中環) movement. The initiative proposed a campaign of planned civil disobedience to pressure the Beijing and Hong Kong governments for democratic electoral reforms. While supporters viewed the movement as a legitimate assertion of civic rights for genuine universal suffrage, critics and government officials condemned it as an unlawful disruption of public order that violated the rule of law. The campaign ultimately merged into the wider **79-day Umbrella Movement** (雨傘運動) in **late 2014**.

## The Hong Kong 47 Trial

In **2020**, **Tai** orchestrated an unofficial primary election for the pro-democracy camp to maximize their chances of winning a legislative majority. Authorities subsequently arrested **Tai** and 46 other pro-democracy politicians and activists under the **National Security Law** enacted in **2020**. Supporters viewed the primaries as a standard political maneuver within a constitutional framework, whereas the prosecution argued the primary was part of a subversive plot to paralyze the government. In **2024**, **Tai** was convicted and sentenced to **10 years in prison** in the landmark **Hong Kong 47 Trial**.
