## Early Survey Work
**Captain Henry Kellett** (1806–1875) was a Royal Navy officer who played a significant role in the hydrographic surveys of the South China Sea and the waters surrounding Hong Kong in the early **1840s**. Commanding the HMS *Starling* and later the HMS *Sulphur*, **Kellett** provided essential navigation data that supported British naval operations during the First Opium War. His name was subsequently given to several geographic features in the region, reflecting the British colonial practice of memorializing officials and naval personnel.

## Kellett Island
**Kellett Island** (奇力島) is the most prominent landmark named in his honor. Located off the coast of East Point on Hong Kong Island, it served as a significant site for both colonial administration and social life. In the **20th century**, the island became the headquarters for the Royal Hong Kong Yacht Club. 

## Historical Interpretations
