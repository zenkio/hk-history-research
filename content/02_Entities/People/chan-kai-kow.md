## Role in the Central School

**Chan Kai Kow** (陳啟鎏) is primarily recognized in the history of Hong Kong education as one of the earliest students of the Government Central School—the institution that succeeded the Morrison Education Society School and the Anglo-Chinese College. Established in **1862**, the Central School was intended to provide a standardized education in both English and Chinese, fostering a class of intermediaries between the British colonial administration and the local Chinese population.
