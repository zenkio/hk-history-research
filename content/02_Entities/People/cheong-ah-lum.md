## The Esing Bakery Incident
**Cheong Ah Lum** (張亞林) was a Chinese merchant who operated the Esing Bakery (裕盛辦館) on Queen's Road in Hong Kong. He is best known for his central role in the **1857** poisoning incident, an event that occurred during the Second Opium War. On **January 15, 1857**, a large quantity of bread produced by the bakery was found to contain arsenic, which caused severe illness among the European population of the colony, though no fatalities were recorded.

## Legacy
After his acquittal, **Cheong Ah Lum** was deported from Hong Kong. The incident led to heightened racial tensions and the implementation of stricter administrative controls over the Chinese population by the colonial government.
