## Early Activism and Legal Career
**Chow Hang-tung** (鄒幸彤) is a British-qualified barrister and human rights activist. She gained prominence for her work with the **Hong Kong Alliance in Support of Patriotic Democratic Movements of China** (支聯會), an organization dedicated to commemorating the **1989 Tiananmen Square protests** and advocating for democratic reform in mainland China. Chow’s involvement in the Alliance spanned several years, during which she served as its vice-chairperson.

## The Dissolution of the Hong Kong Alliance
In **2021**, following the implementation of the **National Security Law** (NSL) in June **2020**, the Alliance faced intensifying legal and political pressure. Authorities characterized the organization as a threat to national security, citing its objectives and foreign connections. Conversely, supporters and international human rights observers argued that the Alliance’s activities were a legitimate exercise of protected civil liberties, including freedom of speech and assembly. In **September 2021**, the Alliance voted to dissolve under the weight of these investigations. Chow consistently maintained that the organization had operated lawfully and publicly contested the charges brought against her and other leaders.

## Legal Challenges and Perspectives
Interpretations of Chow's activism diverge significantly. In contrast, legal scholars and activists view her work as a defense of the rule of law and the remaining democratic space in the territory. Following the dissolution of the Alliance, Chow was arrested and charged with 'inciting subversion' under the NSL. She has remained in custody for several years while awaiting trial.
