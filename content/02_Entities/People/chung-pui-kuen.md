## Career and Journalism
**Chung Pui-kuen** (鍾沛權) served as the editor-in-chief of **Stand News** (立場新聞), a non-profit online news organization founded in 2014 following the Umbrella Movement. Under his leadership, the outlet became known for its extensive coverage of local social issues, political developments, and the 2019 anti-extradition bill protests. Supporters viewed the publication as a critical platform for independent journalism and local voices, while critics and government authorities characterized its editorial stance as biased or, in some cases, harmful to national security.

## Legal Proceedings
In **December 2021**, **Chung Pui-kuen** was arrested alongside other staff members following a police raid on the **Stand News** offices, which subsequently led to the permanent closure of the outlet. He was charged with conspiracy to publish and reproduce seditious publications under the Crimes Ordinance.
