## Early Life and Professional Career

**Leung Chun-ying** (**梁振英**), commonly known as **CY Leung**, was born in Hong Kong in **1954**. A professional surveyor by training, he studied in the United Kingdom before returning to Hong Kong to pursue a career in real estate and land surveying. He rose to prominence through various public advisory bodies, serving as the convenor of the Executive Council under **Tung Chee-hwa** and **Donald Tsang**.

## Chief Executive Term (2012–2017)

In **2012**, **CY Leung** defeated **Henry Tang** (**唐英年**) in the Chief Executive election, securing **689** votes from the Election Committee. His administration faced profound governance challenges. Critics often point to his housing policies and a perceived tightening of Beijing's grip on local affairs as catalysts for rising social discontent. Supporters, conversely, credit him with pragmatism in tackling livelihood issues such as housing shortages and poverty alleviation, as well as initiating the New Territories North development studies.

## HKTV License Controversy

During his tenure in **2013**, the Executive Council denied a free-to-air television license to **Hong Kong Television Network** (**香港電視網絡**), led by **Ricky Wong** (**王維基**). This decision sparked significant public backlash and protests, with critics accusing the government of suppressing media pluralism and political dissent. The administration maintained that the decision followed a comprehensive, objective assessment based on the statutory guidelines and consultant reports regarding the sustainable capacity of the local broadcasting market.

## Later Roles

After stepping down in **2017**, **CY Leung** assumed the role of Vice-Chairman of the National Committee of the Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference (**全國政協副主席**). He remains active in public commentary, frequently advocating for deeper integration between Hong Kong and the **Mainland** through the **Greater Bay Area** initiative.
