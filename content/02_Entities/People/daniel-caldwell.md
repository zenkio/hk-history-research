## Early Career and Influence

**Daniel Caldwell** (高德) arrived in Hong Kong during the early years of British colonial rule. Due to his fluency in Chinese dialects and extensive local connections, he rose to influential positions, including Registrar-General and Protector of Chinese. His intimate knowledge of local society made him indispensable to the early colonial administration.

## The Caldwell-Anstey Scandal

Caldwell's career became deeply controversial during the **Caldwell-Anstey corruption scandal** of **1858** to **1859**. Attorney-General **Thomas Chisholm Anstey** accused Caldwell of widespread corruption, extortion, and collusion with Chinese criminal syndicates, notably the piracy networks led by **Ma Chow Tong** (馬週棠).
