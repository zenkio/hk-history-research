## Role in the Battle of Hong Kong

**Doi Takahiko** (土井市之進) was a Lieutenant Colonel in the Imperial Japanese Army, serving as the commander of the 228th Regiment of the 38th Division during the **December 1941** invasion of Hong Kong. He is most frequently cited in historical accounts regarding the assault on the **Gin Drinkers Line**, a defensive perimeter established by British colonial forces across the Kowloon Peninsula.

## The Breach of the Gin Drinkers Line

On the night of **December 9–10, 1941**, forces under the command of **Doi Takahiko** executed a successful infiltration and subsequent capture of the Shing Mun Redoubt. The rapid fall of this strategic point is often viewed as a pivotal moment in the defense of the colony.

From the perspective of British military records, the fall of the Redoubt was attributed to a combination of Japanese tactical aggression and the logistical challenges faced by the defenders in maintaining such an extensive line with limited personnel.
