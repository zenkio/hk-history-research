## Early Career and Colonial Service

**Donald Tsang** (曾蔭權) joined the British colonial civil service in **1967**, rising through the ranks to become the first Chinese Financial Secretary in **1995**, a pivotal appointment leading up to the **1997** handover. During the **1997** Asian Financial Crisis, as Financial Secretary, he played a central role in the **Defense of the Hong Kong Dollar Peg**, executing controversial government interventions in the stock market to deter speculative attacks.

Tsang continued to serve in the post-handover administration under **Tung Chee-hwa** (董建華), eventually succeeding **Anson Chan** (陳方安生) as Chief Secretary for Administration in **2001**. ## Chief Executive Tenure and Legacy

Interpretations of Tsang's legacy vary.
