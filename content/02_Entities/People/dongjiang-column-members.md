## Historical Context
The **Dongjiang Column** (東江縱隊), formally the Guangdong People's Anti-Japanese Guerrilla Force, was a communist-led resistance group that operated primarily in the Pearl River Delta and the **New Territories** (新界) of Hong Kong during the **Japanese Occupation** (1941–1945). Following the fall of Hong Kong in **December 1941**, the group established the Hong Kong-Kowloon Independent Brigade (港九大隊).

## Roles and Operations
The members played a critical role in the "Operation of the Secret Underground News Network," providing intelligence on Japanese troop movements and maintaining communications. They are perhaps best known for the "Short-term Rescue" (營救文化人), an operation that successfully escorted hundreds of Chinese intellectuals, cultural figures, and their families—including **Soong Ching-ling** (宋慶齡)—through Japanese lines to safety in free China.

## Differing Historical Perspectives
Interpretations of the group's legacy vary significantly. **Beijing** and official PRC accounts highlight the column as a heroic, patriotic force that spearheaded the resistance against Japanese imperialism in the region. In contrast, **British colonial** historical accounts often framed the group’s presence as an encroachment on sovereignty, focusing on their post-war transition into political activists rather than solely resistance fighters. Among **local New Territories communities**, memories remain complex; while some villagers provided supplies and shelter, others were caught in the crossfire of the guerrillas' skirmishes with Japanese forces. Later scholarship seeks to reconcile these narratives, acknowledging the group’s tactical effectiveness in the anti-Japanese war while documenting the long-term socio-political impact of their underground networks on Hong Kong's internal security landscape.

## Timeline

- **1941**: Formation of the Hong Kong-Kowloon Independent Brigade following the fall of Hong Kong.
- **1942**: Successful rescue operation of Chinese intellectuals from occupied Hong Kong.
- **1943**: Formal establishment of the Dongjiang Column in Guangdong.
- **1945**: Withdrawal of forces from Hong Kong following the Japanese surrender.
