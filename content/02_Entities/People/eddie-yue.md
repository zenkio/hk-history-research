## Background and Career

**Eddie Yue** (余偉文) has spent decades within the financial governance structure of Hong Kong. He joined the Hong Kong Government as an Administrative Officer in **1986** and later moved to the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA) upon its establishment in **1993**. Over the years, he ascended through various senior management positions, eventually succeeding **Norman Chan** (陳德霖) as the Chief Executive of the HKMA in **October 2019**.
