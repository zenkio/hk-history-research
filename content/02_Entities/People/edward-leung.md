## Background and Political Rise
**Edward Leung** (梁天琦) gained national attention as the spokesperson for the localist group **Hong Kong Indigenous** (本土民主前線). He became a central figure in the discourse surrounding Hong Kong's political identity, advocating for self-determination and the preservation of local customs. In **2016**, he participated in the Legislative Council by-election for the New Territories East constituency, where his performance highlighted a growing segment of the electorate favoring more radical or localized political platforms.

## The Mong Kok Clash
Leung is most notably associated with the **2016 Mong Kok civil unrest**, often referred to by participants as the "Fishball Revolution" (魚蛋革命). The events began on the night of **February 8, 2016**, following attempts by authorities to clear unauthorized street hawkers during the Lunar New Year. Tensions escalated into clashes between protesters and police. Supporters of the movement framed the protest as an act of resistance against the suppression of local heritage and state overreach. Conversely, government authorities and pro-establishment commentators characterized the events as a riot involving illegal violence that threatened public order.

In **2018**, Leung was convicted of rioting and assaulting a police officer, receiving a six-year prison sentence.
