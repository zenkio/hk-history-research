## Historical Significance
**Emperor Gaozu of Southern Han**, born **Liu Yan** (劉龑), was the founder of the **Southern Han** (南漢) dynasty, which ruled the Lingnan region during the **Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms** period. He reigned from **917** to **942** and is historically significant to the Hong Kong region for his assertion of administrative control over the coastal areas of modern-day Guangdong, including the strategic port of **Tuen Mun** (屯門).

## Connection to Hong Kong
Under the **Southern Han** administration, the government took a proactive interest in the maritime trade routes connecting Guangzhou to the South China Sea. Historians generally agree that the **Tuen Mun** area served as a critical naval outpost and a commercial gateway. Records from the period suggest the establishment of military garrisons to protect trade interests, though the exact extent of this infrastructure remains a subject of archaeological debate.

## Historiographical Perspectives
Interpretations regarding **Liu Yan**’s rule vary across different academic and political traditions. **Qing dynasty** and traditional historiography often emphasized the period as one of regional fragmentation, viewing the Southern Han as a tributary regime. Conversely, some modern scholars and **local New Territories** historical perspectives highlight the dynasty's role in the early integration of the Lingnan region into the broader economic landscape of East Asia, suggesting that the development of coastal settlements like Tuen Mun laid the groundwork for later maritime expansion.

While **British colonial**-era records sometimes downplayed the administrative sophistication of the Southern Han, contemporary archaeological findings in the Pearl River Delta suggest a more complex, structured society than earlier narratives proposed. Whether the Southern Han exerted direct, centralized control over the entirety of the modern Hong Kong territory remains uncertain, with scholars noting that imperial authority likely relied on a mix of local military presence and the management of maritime trade guilds.

- **917**: Liu Yan declares himself Emperor and establishes the Southern Han dynasty.

- **942**: Death of Emperor Gaozu; the Southern Han regime continues until 971.
