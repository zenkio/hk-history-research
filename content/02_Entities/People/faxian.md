## Background and Pilgrimage

**Faxian** (**法顯**, c. **337**–c. **422** CE) was a Chinese Buddhist monk who set out from Chang'an in **399** CE to travel to India in search of Buddhist scriptures. After spending over a decade gathering texts, he began his return journey to China by sea in the early 5th century, boarding merchant ships from the Indian subcontinent through Southeast Asia.
