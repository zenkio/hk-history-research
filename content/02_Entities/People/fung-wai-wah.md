## Background and Leadership

**Fung Wai-wah** (馮偉華) emerged as a key figure in Hong Kong's education sector, eventually taking on the presidency of the **Hong Kong Professional Teachers' Union** (PTU) (香港教育專業人員協會). The PTU was historically the largest single-industry union in Hong Kong, wielding significant influence not only in professional educational matters concerning teachers' welfare and curriculum development but also within the broader pro-democracy political landscape.

## The Disbanding of the PTU

During Fung's tenure, the political environment in Hong Kong underwent profound shifts following the enactment of the **National Security Law** in **2020**. State-run media in mainland China and pro-establishment figures in Hong Kong increasingly targeted the PTU, labeling it a "poisonous" organization and accusing it of inciting anti-government sentiments among youth.

Interpretations of these events diverge sharply. Pro-establishment and Beijing perspectives framed the dissolution of the PTU as a necessary correction to restore professional standards and national security in education, arguing the union had strayed far from its professional mandate into radical politics. Conversely, pro-democracy supporters, union members, and international observers viewed the pressure placed on Fung and the PTU as a severe blow to civil society, academic freedom, and labor rights in Hong Kong.

Fung and the union leadership ultimately announced the disbandment of the PTU in **August 2021**, citing the rapidly changing political climate and safety concerns for its members. This brought an end to decades of organized teacher activism in the territory.
