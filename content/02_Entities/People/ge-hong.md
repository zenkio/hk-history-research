## Historical Background and Lingnan Connection

While his historical footprint spans much of southern China, local tradition and later scholarship heavily associate him with the Lingnan region, which encompasses modern-day Guangdong and Hong Kong. **Ge Hong** is traditionally believed to have travelled extensively through southern coastal areas in search of cinnabar and other minerals required for alchemical elixir production.
