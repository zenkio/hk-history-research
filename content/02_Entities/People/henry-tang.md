## Early Career and Political Rise
Born in **1952**, **Henry Tang** (唐英年) is a scion of a prominent textile industrialist family. He entered the Legislative Council in **1991** representing the industrial functional constituency. Following the **1997** handover, he was appointed to the Executive Council, later serving as Secretary for Commerce, Industry and Technology in **2002**. In **2007**, he was appointed Chief Secretary for Administration by Chief Executive **Donald Tsang** (曾蔭權).

## The 2012 Chief Executive Election
**Henry Tang** is perhaps best remembered for his participation in the **2012** Chief Executive election. He was initially viewed by many as the preferred candidate of the establishment. However, his campaign was significantly impacted by the revelation of unauthorized building works at his family home in Kowloon Tong. During the campaign, his rivalry with **Leung Chun-ying** (梁振英) became a focal point of public discourse, highlighting deep divisions regarding the influence of the business elite versus the perceived populist approach of his opponent. Ultimately, **Leung Chun-ying** emerged victorious.

His defeat in **2012** is frequently cited by political analysts as a turning point in Hong Kong’s political climate, signaling a shift away from the traditional, consensus-based selection of leaders favored by the city's business conglomerates, though the extent to which this signaled a long-term change in political dynamics remains a subject of ongoing historical debate.
