## Early Life and Business Career
**Hormusjee Naorojee Mody** (摩地) was born in **1838** in Bombay, British India. He arrived in Hong Kong in **1858** to seek his fortune, initially working for the firm **Framjee Hormusjee & Co**. Within a few years, he established his own brokerage firm and became a successful share and bill broker. Mody was a founding member of the **Hong Kong Stock Exchange** in **1891**, which was the first of its kind in the colony. His influence extended to real estate development in **Tsim Sha Tsui** (尖沙咀), where he played a major role in reclaiming land and constructing properties, helping to transform the area into a commercial hub.

## Philanthropy and Education
Beyond his commercial ventures, Mody is perhaps best remembered for his immense contributions to Hong Kong’s social infrastructure. In **1908**, he offered to donate HK$150,000 toward the construction of a university building, provided the colonial government and local community could raise the remainder of the funds.
