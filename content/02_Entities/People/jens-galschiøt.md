The eight-meter-tall sculpture, featuring distorted human bodies, was designed to symbolize the suffering and loss of life associated with the **1989 Tiananmen Square protests**. 

## Historical Context and Interpretations
In **1997**, the sculpture was brought to Hong Kong and displayed at the University of Hong Kong (HKU) to commemorate the victims of the **June 4th incident**.
