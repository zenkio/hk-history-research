Following his tenure as Minister of Foreign Affairs, **Ji** was appointed as the Chairman of the Drafting Committee for the Basic Law of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region in **1985**. In this capacity, he oversaw the formulation of the constitutional document intended to govern Hong Kong after the **1997** transfer of sovereignty.
