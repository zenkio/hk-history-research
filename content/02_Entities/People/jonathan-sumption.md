## Background and Appointment

**Jonathan Sumption** (岑耀信) is a distinguished British legal scholar, historian, and former Justice of the Supreme Court of the United Kingdom. In **2019**, he was appointed as a non-permanent judge of the Court of Final Appeal in Hong Kong. This appointment was part of a long-standing practice under the Basic Law, intended to maintain confidence in Hong Kong's common law system by including experienced overseas jurists in the highest appellate court.

## Resignation and Differing Perspectives

His departure has sparked ongoing debate among legal scholars regarding the future efficacy of the overseas judge scheme and the extent to which the judiciary can remain isolated from the political developments impacting the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
