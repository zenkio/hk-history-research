## Accession and Early Reign
The **Kangxi Emperor** (康熙帝) ruled the Qing dynasty from **1661** to **1722**. Early in his reign, the Qing court faced the ongoing threat of the Southern Ming loyalist **Koxinga** (鄭成功), who operated from Taiwan. To sever local supply lines and maritime support for anti-Qing resistance along the southern coast, the imperial government enforced the Great Clearance (**遷界**), which forcibly evacuated coastal populations inland.

## Impact on the Hong Kong Region
The coastal evacuation heavily impacted the territory that now forms Hong Kong. Settlements across the New Territories and coastal areas were abandoned, and local inhabitants suffered immense disruption. Traditional historiography and local lineage traditions emphasize the devastation this brought to agrarian and fishing communities. However, interpretations differ regarding the primary motivations and immediate local enforcement; some local and community perspectives view it as a blunt, devastating instrument of imperial control, while Qing official records framed it as a necessary military sacrifice for state security.

## Resettlement and Hakka Immigration
By **1669**, with the anti-Qing threat receding, the Kangxi Emperor approved the Rescission of the Great Clearance (**展界**), allowing displaced residents to return. Due to slow natural repopulating of the devastated lands, subsequent Qing encouragement and tax incentives facilitated the influx of new settlers, notably Hakka (**客家**) communities from inland provinces. Later scholarship highlights how this resettlement initiative permanently altered the ethnolinguistic fabric of the region, introducing the dual Punti (**本地**) and Hakka demographic dynamic that characterized the New Territories for centuries.

## Timeline

- **1661**: The Kangxi Emperor ascends the Qing throne.
- **1662**: Implementation of the Great Clearance severely affects the coastal Hong Kong region.
- **1669**: The Kangxi Emperor officially permits the Rescission of the Great Clearance, allowing return and resettlement.
- **1722**: The Kangxi Emperor dies after a 61-year reign.
