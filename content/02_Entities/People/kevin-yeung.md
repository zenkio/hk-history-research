## Career and Educational Policy

**Kevin Yeung Yun-hung** (楊潤雄) joined the Hong Kong Government as an Administrative Officer in **1992**. Over the following decades, he held various positions across several bureaus. In **2012**, he was appointed Under Secretary for Education, eventually serving as the Secretary for Education from **2017** to **2022**.

## The New Liberal Studies Curriculum

Yeung’s tenure as Secretary for Education was marked by significant controversy and debate regarding the Liberal Studies curriculum, a core subject introduced in **2009**. Proponents of the original curriculum argued that it encouraged critical thinking and civic engagement among students. Conversely, critics—including members of the pro-establishment camp and officials in Beijing—contended that the subject lacked sufficient oversight and potentially contributed to the politicization of youth, especially following the social unrest in **2014** and **2019**.

Under Yeung’s leadership, the Education Bureau announced a major overhaul of the subject. In **2021**, the government renamed the subject to "Citizenship and Social Development" and shifted the focus toward national security, national identity, and the rule of law. 

## Interpretations of Curriculum Reform

Interpretations of these reforms remain divided. Government supporters maintain that the changes were necessary to rectify "misguided" pedagogy and to ensure that the education system aligns with the constitutional reality of Hong Kong as a Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China. In contrast, some educationalists, local concern groups, and overseas observers argue that these shifts represent a contraction of academic freedom and a deliberate effort to dampen political dissent within the classroom. Whether these changes will achieve the government's stated goals of fostering patriotism or lead to long-term shifts in student engagement with civic issues remains a subject of ongoing historical and sociological assessment.

Following his tenure at the Education Bureau, Yeung was appointed Secretary for Culture, Sports and Tourism in **2022** under the administration of **John Lee Ka-chiu** (李家超).
