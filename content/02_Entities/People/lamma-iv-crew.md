## Overview

The **Lamma IV crew** operated the vessel owned by **Hong Kong Electric** (香港電燈) during the tragic collision on **October 1, 2012**. The passenger ferry, carrying employees and their families to view the National Day fireworks, was struck by the **Hong Kong & Kowloon Ferry** vessel **Sea Smooth** (海泰號) near **Lamma Island** (南丫島).

## The Collision and Investigation

The incident resulted in **39 fatalities**, making it one of the worst maritime disasters in Hong Kong's recent history. Subsequent investigations and court proceedings scrutinized the actions, training, and safety compliance of the **Lamma IV crew** alongside the crew of the Sea Smooth. Particular focus was placed on maritime navigation rules, the failure to maintain a proper lookout, and the notable absence of life jackets suitable for children on board the vessel.

## Public and Legal Discourse

Legal proceedings examined the division of responsibility between the two vessels and the regulatory oversight of the **Marine Department** (海事處). While official inquiries highlighted multiple systemic and operational failures across both ship crews and regulatory bodies, public discussion in Hong Kong often reflected deep sorrow for the victims and intense scrutiny regarding corporate safety standards and government enforcement of maritime regulations.
