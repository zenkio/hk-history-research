## Background and Disappearance
**Lee Bo** (李波) was a key figure associated with Causeway Bay Books (銅鑼灣書店), a publishing house in Hong Kong known for selling books critical of the Chinese Communist Party leadership. On **December 30, 2015**, **Lee Bo** went missing from Hong Kong, becoming the fifth individual associated with the bookstore to disappear within a short period. Unlike his associates, who had disappeared in mainland China or Thailand, **Lee Bo** was last seen at a warehouse in Chai Wan, Hong Kong, leading to widespread speculation that he had been abducted by mainland Chinese security agents operating extra-territorially.

## Differing Interpretations
Interpretations of the event vary significantly. Pro-democracy activists and many in the Hong Kong legal community argued that the incident represented a direct violation of the Basic Law (基本法), suggesting that mainland law enforcement had bypassed the territory's jurisdiction. They viewed the case as a severe erosion of the 'One Country, Two Systems' (一國兩制) principle, which guarantees Hong Kong a high degree of autonomy.

Conversely, official statements from mainland authorities initially maintained that **Lee Bo** was a Chinese national who had 'voluntarily' traveled to the mainland to assist in an investigation. Following the incident, **Lee Bo** appeared on mainland state television stating he had gone to China of his own volition to cooperate with authorities, though critics and some international human rights groups questioned the circumstances under which such statements were made. Later, after returning to Hong Kong, he briefly resumed his life before requesting that the police drop the investigation into his disappearance, citing that the matter was resolved. The incident remains a touchstone in discussions regarding the shifting political climate in Hong Kong during the mid-2010s.
