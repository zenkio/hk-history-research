A graduate of the University of Hong Kong, he became deeply involved in the Christian Industrial Committee. ## Political Career
Lee served as a member of the **Legislative Council** of Hong Kong from **1995** to **2016**, representing the Labour functional constituency. As a founding member of the **Confederation of Trade Unions** (職工盟), he advocated for workers' rights, including the implementation of the minimum wage and collective bargaining laws. His political identity is closely tied to the pro-democracy camp, where he was consistently re-elected until his departure from the legislature.
