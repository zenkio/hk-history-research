## Early Life and Rise to Stardom

Born in Beijing in **1966**, **Leon Lai** (黎明) moved to Hong Kong during his youth. He entered the entertainment industry after winning the New Talent Singing Awards in **1986**. By the early **1990s**, Lai established himself as a major cultural figure in the territory, alongside **Jacky Cheung** (張學友), **Andy Lau** (劉德華), and **Aaron Kwok** (郭富城), collectively dubbed the **'Four Heavenly Kings'** (四大天王) by the media.

## Cantopop and Film Career
