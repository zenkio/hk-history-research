## Early Life and Academic Career

**Lindsay Ride** (賴特) was born in **1898**. Before the outbreak of the Pacific War, he served as a professor of physiology at the **University of Hong Kong** (香港大學). His academic career established him deeply within the local colonial establishment.

## World War II and the British Army Aid Group

Following the Japanese invasion of Hong Kong in **December 1941**, Ride escaped to mainland China. In **1942**, operating under the auspices of the British military, he founded the **British Army Aid Group** (英軍服務團). 

Stationed primarily in free China, the BAAG utilized networks of local villagers, fishermen, and guerrilla fighters to smuggle medicine, food, and intelligence into and out of Japanese-occupied Hong Kong. The organization also facilitated the daring escape of civilian internees, prisoners of war, and allied agents from camps such as **Sham Shui Po** (深水埗). 

## Post-War Contributions
