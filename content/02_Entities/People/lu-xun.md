## Background and Rebellion

**Lu Xun** (盧循) was a military commander and rebel leader during the Eastern Jin dynasty. Alongside his brother-in-law **Qiu Xi** (徐道覆), he led a major uprising against the imperial court, inheriting the remnants of the earlier **Sun En** (孫恩) rebellion. As imperial forces pushed southward, Lu Xun's naval fleet and followers moved along the southern coast of China.
