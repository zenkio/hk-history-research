**Martin Lee** (李柱銘) was born in **1938** and established a successful career as a barrister, eventually becoming a Queen's Counsel (QC) in **1988**. His early prominence in the legal sector paved the way for his entry into public advocacy during the transitional period of Hong Kong's history.

## Drafting the Basic Law

In **1985**, Lee was appointed by Beijing as a member of the **Drafting Committee for the Basic Law** of the future Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
