## Early Life and Political Ascent

Following the movement, he co-founded the political party Demosistō in **2016**.

## Legislative Council and Oath-Taking Controversy
In the **2016** Legislative Council general election, **Law** was elected to represent the Hong Kong Island constituency. His tenure was short-lived due to the **Oath-Taking Controversy**. During his swearing-in ceremony on **October 12, 2016**, he altered the tone and cadence of his oath, leading to a legal challenge initiated by the Hong Kong government. 

Interpretations of these events remain sharply divided. In **July 2017**, a court ruled that his oath was invalid, leading to his disqualification from the legislature.

## Subsequent Exile and Global Advocacy

In **July 2020**, the Hong Kong police issued an arrest warrant for him on suspicion of inciting secession and collusion with foreign forces. While international observers and some Western governments have frequently cited his advocacy as a matter of human rights and civil liberties, the Hong Kong and mainland Chinese authorities maintain that his activities constitute a threat to national security and order.
