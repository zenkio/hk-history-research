Interpretations of the exact timing and motivations behind the Ng clan's arrival vary between local lineage accounts and broader historical scholarship. 

* **Local Community Tradition:** Village genealogies often emphasize ancestral lineages tracing back to Song dynasty officials or scholars who fled southward due to dynastic turmoil and Mongol invasions. These accounts highlight the spiritual and territorial continuity maintained through ancestral halls.

The settlement of Ha Tsuen and surrounding areas involved systematic land reclamation, the establishment of walled villages (wai), and the cultivation of paddy fields, often in cooperation or competition with neighboring clans like the **Tang (鄧氏)**.
