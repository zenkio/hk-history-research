## Historical Origins and Settlement
The **Pang Clan** (彭氏) are recognized as one of the Five Great Clans of the New Territories. According to genealogical traditions maintained by the clan, their ancestors migrated from the north to the Hong Kong region during the **Song Dynasty** (960–1279 AD). The precise timeline of their initial arrival remains a subject of ongoing historical discussion, as records from this period were often lost or damaged during the Ming Dynasty evacuation of the coastal regions.

## The Establishment of Fanling Wai
The most significant historical site associated with the **Pang Clan Ancestors** is **Fanling Wai** (粉嶺圍). While clan records frequently cite the late **Southern Song Dynasty** or early **Yuan Dynasty** (1271–1368 AD) as the period of settlement, some modern archaeological scholars suggest that the consolidation of the current walled village structure occurred later, likely during the **Ming Dynasty** (1368–1644 AD) when security in the region necessitated more formal defensive architecture. The village is notable for its traditional layout, including the central shrine and the pond that reflects the clan's ancestral commitment to *feng shui* and defensive planning.

## Differing Historical Perspectives
