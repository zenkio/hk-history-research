## Career and Stand News
**Patrick Lam** served as the chief editor of **Stand News** (立場新聞), a prominent digital-native media outlet in Hong Kong known for its coverage of social movements and political affairs. Following the closure of **Apple Daily** in mid-2021, **Stand News** became one of the few remaining outlets providing consistent reporting on the city's shifting political environment. 

## The December 2021 Raid and Legal Proceedings
On **December 29, 2021**, Hong Kong national security police conducted a raid on the offices of **Stand News**, leading to the arrests of several current and former staff members, including **Patrick Lam**. Following the arrests, **Stand News** announced the immediate cessation of its operations and the deletion of its digital archives. 

Legal proceedings followed, with authorities charging the outlet and its leadership with conspiracy to publish seditious materials under the colonial-era Crimes Ordinance. The prosecution argued that several articles published by the outlet incited hatred against the government and undermined national security. Conversely, press freedom advocates and international human rights organizations raised concerns that the prosecution represented a significant contraction of media plurality in Hong Kong. 

In **August 2024**, a District Court judge delivered a verdict in the sedition case. **Patrick Lam** was found guilty, though the court noted his health condition during the proceedings. The case remains a focal point for scholarly and public debate regarding the evolving definitions of press freedom and the application of security laws in post-2020 Hong Kong. While the government maintains that these legal actions are necessary to uphold the rule of law and maintain social stability, critics contend that such measures have fundamentally altered the landscape for independent journalism in the city.
