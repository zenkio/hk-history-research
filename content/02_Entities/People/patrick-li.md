## Background and Appointment

**Patrick Li** (李百全) is a career administrative officer in the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region government. In **March 2021**, he was appointed as the Director of Broadcasting, succeeding **Leung Ka-wing** (梁家榮). His appointment came at a time when the operations and editorial line of the public broadcaster, Radio Television Hong Kong (RTHK), were facing intense scrutiny from pro-Beijing figures, local media, and government officials regarding compliance with the Basic Law and the principle of "patriots administering Hong Kong."

## Revamping of Public Broadcasting Guidelines

Upon taking office, Li initiated sweeping changes to RTHK's management, programming, and administrative procedures. Under his leadership, the broadcaster introduced the **Revamping of Public Broadcasting Guidelines for RTHK**, aimed at aligning its output more closely with the official editorial standards expected of a government department. Several ongoing or completed television and radio programs were pulled from broadcast or online platforms prior to public release, leading to debates over editorial independence and freedom of expression.
