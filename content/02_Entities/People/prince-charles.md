## Role in the Transfer of Sovereignty

**Prince Charles** (查爾斯王子), now **King Charles III**, served as the official representative of **Queen Elizabeth II** at the **Transfer of Sovereignty over Hong Kong** on **June 30, 1997**. He arrived in Hong Kong aboard the royal yacht *Britannia* to preside over the **Sunset Farewell Ceremony**, which signaled the conclusion of over 150 years of British administration.

Later scholarship often analyzes the event through the lens of post-colonial theory, debating whether the handover truly represented a clean break from the past or the continuation of complex political and economic legacies.

While he has not played a direct role in Hong Kong affairs since his accession to the throne in **2022**, his participation in the 1997 handover remains a defining historical image of the end of the British Empire in Asia.
