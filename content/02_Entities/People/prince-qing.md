## Role in the Second Convention of Peking
**Prince Qing** (慶親王), born **Yikuang** (奕劻), served as a senior statesman in the late Qing court. His historical significance regarding Hong Kong centers on his participation in the diplomacy surrounding the **Second Convention of Peking** (展拓香港界址專條), signed in **1898**. As the representative of the Qing government, he was tasked with navigating the expansion of the British colonial presence in the region.
