## Early Activism and Hong Kong Indigenous
**Ray Wong** (黃台仰) gained prominence following the 2014 Umbrella Movement. In 2015, he co-founded the localist political group **Hong Kong Indigenous** (本土民主前線). The group advocated for the protection of local culture and greater autonomy, frequently engaging in protests against parallel trading in northern districts. His political stance marked a departure from traditional pan-democratic strategies, favoring more assertive tactics in public demonstrations.

## The 2016 Mong Kok Clash
On the night of **February 8, 2016**, and into the early hours of **February 9, 2016**, a series of confrontations occurred in **Mong Kok** (旺角) following an attempt by authorities to clear unlicensed street hawkers. The event, often referred to by supporters as the "Fishball Revolution" (魚蛋革命) and by government authorities as a "riot," resulted in violent clashes between protesters and police. **Ray Wong** was present during the events and was subsequently arrested in February 2016 on charges of rioting and inciting others to riot. The incident remains a subject of intense debate; some observers describe it as a manifestation of social frustration and a defense of local heritage, while government and official accounts categorize it as a criminal breakdown of public order and the rule of law.

## Exile and Current Status

In **2019**, it was confirmed that he had been granted refugee status by the German government, making him the first Hong Kong activist to receive such status. The Hong Kong government expressed strong opposition to this decision, while international human rights observers have frequently cited his case in discussions regarding the changing political climate in the territory.
