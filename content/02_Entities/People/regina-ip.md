## Early Career and Security Portfolio
**Regina Ip** (葉劉淑儀) joined the Hong Kong civil service in **1975**. She rose through the ranks, eventually becoming the first woman to be appointed as the Secretary for Security in **1998**, a position she held following the **1997** handover of Hong Kong to the People's Republic of China. Her tenure was marked by a firm approach to public order and governance, which garnered praise from those prioritizing stability but invited scrutiny from civil libertarians.

## The Article 23 Controversy
In **2002**, **Ip** became the public face of the government’s efforts to introduce legislation for Article 23 of the **Basic Law**, which mandated that Hong Kong enact laws to prohibit acts of treason, secession, sedition, and subversion against the Central People's Government. The proposal sparked intense debate. Pro-establishment supporters viewed the bill as a necessary constitutional obligation to protect national security. Conversely, democracy advocates and civil society groups feared the legislation would curtail fundamental rights and freedoms, viewing it as a potential tool for suppressing dissent.

## The 2003 Mass Mobilization
The tension surrounding the Article 23 bill reached a zenith on **July 1, 2003**, the sixth anniversary of the handover. An estimated 500,000 protestors took to the streets in what became the largest demonstration since **1997**. While supporters of the government argued the protest was driven by a confluence of economic grievances and misunderstanding of the bill's safeguards, critics argued it was a clear public rejection of the administration's authoritarian direction. Following the rally and the subsequent resignation of Financial Secretary **Antony Leung** and her own resignation in **July 2003**, the government indefinitely shelved the bill. **Ip** later sought further education in the United States before returning to local politics, eventually founding the **New People's Party** (新民黨) in **2011** and continuing her career as a **Legislative Council** member and **Executive Council** convenor.
