## Introduction and Arrival

The **Salesians of Don Bosco** (慈幼會), formally known as the Society of Saint Francis de Sales, are a Catholic religious institute founded by **St. John Bosco** in Italy. The congregation arrived in Hong Kong in **1927**, responding to requests to expand their educational and charitable mission in East Asia.

## The Aberdeen Industrial School

The most notable early contribution of the Salesians to Hong Kong was their assumption and expansion of the **Aberdeen Industrial School** (香港仔工業學校), originally established by the **Société des Missions Étrangères de Paris** (巴黎外方傳教會). Under Salesian management, the institution provided destitute and orphaned boys with rigorous academic instruction alongside practical vocational training in trades such as printing, carpentry, and shoemaking. 

## Historiographical Perspectives

Assessments of the Salesians' early institutional role vary across different historical frameworks:

* **Colonial and Church Historiography:** British colonial reports and Catholic Church records generally emphasize the civilizing and stabilizing mission of the Salesians. They are credited with mitigating urban poverty, reducing juvenile delinquency, and supplying a disciplined, skilled labor force to support Hong Kong's growing industrial economy.
* **Local and Critical Scholarship:** Later social historians often contextualize these missionary efforts within the broader framework of European imperialism and paternalistic social control. While acknowledging the tangible welfare benefits provided to marginalized youth, contemporary analyses examine how religious institutions collaborated with or filled gaps left by the colonial government in social administration, shaping the moral and social order of the New Territories and urban Kowloon and Hong Kong Island.

Despite differing interpretations, the Salesians established a lasting foundation for technical education in the territory, paving the way for numerous secondary schools and vocational institutes that persist today.
