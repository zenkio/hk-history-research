## Governor of Hong Kong

**Sir George William Des Voeux** (德輔) served as the Governor of Hong Kong from **1887** to **1891**. During his tenure, the colony experienced rapid urban development and infrastructural modernization, aimed at accommodating a growing population and expanding commercial interests.

## The Peak Tramway and Infrastructure

One of the notable events during his administration was the official opening of the **Peak Tramway** in **1888**. While colonial authorities viewed the funicular railway as a triumph of British engineering that facilitated residential expansion to the cooler Victoria Peak, local communities and later scholarship often contextualize this within broader patterns of social segregation, as the Peak increasingly became an exclusive enclave for the European elite.

## Historiographical Perspectives

British colonial historiography traditionally praised **Des Voeux** for his administrative competence and contributions to Hong Kong's economic foundations. In contrast, modern scholarship and contemporary local perspectives sometimes emphasize the stark social stratification of the period. Debates persist regarding the extent to which late 19th-century infrastructure served the broader populace versus European commercial hegemony.
