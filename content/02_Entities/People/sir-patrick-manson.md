## Early Life and Hong Kong Career

**Sir Patrick Manson** (麥遜) arrived in Hong Kong in **1883** after spending over a decade practicing medicine in Amoy (Xiamen) and Taiwan. During his time in Southern China, Manson made groundbreaking observations regarding filariasis, discovering that mosquitoes acted as vectors for the parasite. Upon settling in Hong Kong, he became a prominent figure in the local medical community and played a pivotal role in establishing formal medical education in the territory, which eventually led to the founding of the Hong Kong College of Medicine for Chinese in **1887**.

## Founding of the Dairy Farm Company

Beyond his contributions to tropical medicine, Manson is widely remembered in Hong Kong economic and social history for co-founding **The Dairy Farm Company** (牛奶公司) in **1886** alongside **Sir Catchick Paul Chater** (遮打). Recognizing the high prevalence of infectious diseases and the lack of a reliable, hygienic milk supply for the growing colonial population, Manson envisioned a local dairy enterprise that could provide safe, affordable milk.
