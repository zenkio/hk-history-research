## Early Life and Education

**Sir Shouson Chow** (周壽臣) was born in **1861** in **Hong Kong**. In his youth, he was selected by the Qing government as part of the Chinese Educational Mission, traveling to the United States to study at **Phillips Academy** in Andover, Massachusetts, and later attending **Columbia University**. ## Colonial Administration and Business

Returning to Hong Kong, Chow entered public service and the corporate sector. His career reached a historic milestone in **1926** when he was appointed to the **Executive Council** (ExCo) by Governor **Sir Cecil Clementi**, making him the first ethnic Chinese person to sit on Hong Kong's highest policymaking body. Concurrently, he served on the **Legislative Council** (LegCo) and directed major enterprises, including the **Hong Kong and Kowloon Wharf and Godown Company**.
