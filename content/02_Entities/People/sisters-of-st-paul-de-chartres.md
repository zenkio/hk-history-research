Originating in France, the congregation arrived in the territory to support the pastoral and charitable needs of the growing Catholic community under British administration.

## Arrival and Early Operations
In **1848**, the first group of sisters arrived in Hong Kong at the invitation of the Roman Catholic Church to assist with orphanages and education.
