## Role in Hong Kong-Mainland Relations
**Song Defu** (宋德福) was a significant figure in the governance of the People's Republic of China during the transition and early post-handover periods of Hong Kong. As a high-ranking official who served as the Minister of Personnel and later as the Party Secretary of Fujian Province, he was deeply involved in the policy frameworks that integrated Hong Kong's economy with that of the Mainland. 

## Contribution to CEPA
**Song Defu** is most frequently cited in the context of the **Closer Economic Partnership Arrangement (CEPA)**. As the bilateral negotiations progressed in the early 2000s, **Song** was instrumental in managing the institutional coordination required to deepen economic ties. From the perspective of the Beijing authorities, his involvement was essential in stabilizing Hong Kong's economy following the 1997 handover and the subsequent Asian Financial Crisis. Scholars often highlight that the institutional architecture established under the guidance of officials like **Song** marked a turning point in how the Central Government viewed the role of Hong Kong as a specialized financial and service gateway for the Mainland economy.
