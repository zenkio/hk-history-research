## Early Life and Political Rise

**Starry Lee** (李慧琼) was born in **1974** and trained as an accountant before entering public service. She joined the **Democratic Alliance for the Betterment and Progress of Hong Kong** (**DAB** 民主建港協進聯盟), the territory's largest pro-establishment political party, establishing herself as a leading voice for the pro-Beijing camp. **Lee** was elected to the **Legislative Council** (**LegCo** 立法會) and later became the chairwoman of the DAB in **2015**, making her the first woman to lead the party.

This miscommunication resulted in a shock **28-8** defeat of the **Beijing-backed electoral reform bill**.
