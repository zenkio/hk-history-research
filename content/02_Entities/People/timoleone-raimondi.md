## Early Life and Arrival

**Timoleone Raimondi** (藍來猷) was born in Italy and became a member of the **Pontifical Institute for Foreign Missions** (PIME). He arrived in **Hong Kong** during the mid-19th century when the British colony was rapidly developing as a strategic port and missionary hub.

## Establishment of the Prefecture Apostolic

Following the detachment of Hong Kong from the Apostolic Prefecture of **Guangdong** and **Guangxi**, Raimondi was appointed to lead the newly formed **Roman Catholic Prefecture Apostolic of Hong Kong** in **1868**, succeeding **Aloysius Ambrosi**. As the ecclesiastical superior, Raimondi oversaw the expansion of Catholic infrastructure, navigating the delicate balance between British colonial authorities, local Chinese communities, and the Qing administration across the border.

## Education and Social Welfare

Historiography generally credits Raimondi with expanding the Church's reach through education and social services. Under his leadership, various religious orders were invited to establish schools, orphanages, and hospitals, which catered to both the European expatriate population and the local Chinese residents. ## Legacy

Raimondi served as Prefect until his death in **1894**. His tenure laid the groundwork for the future Roman Catholic Diocese of Hong Kong, shaping the institutional footprint of the Church in the territory for decades to follow.
