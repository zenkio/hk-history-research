Appointed as the Secretary for Environment and Ecology in **July 2022** under the administration of Chief Executive **John Lee** (李家超), Tse assumed responsibility for steering Hong Kong's policies on climate change, energy, food safety, and environmental hygiene.

## Municipal Waste Charging Scheme

One of the most significant challenges during Tse's tenure involved the legislative and implementation process for the municipal waste charging scheme. Originally formulated years prior to incentivize waste reduction, the policy faced mounting logistical hurdles, public concern over economic impacts, and implementation delays.
