## The Lisbon Maru Incident
The **Lisbon Maru** (里斯本丸) was a Japanese armed freighter transporting approximately 1,800 British prisoners of war from Hong Kong to Japan. On **October 1, 1942**, the vessel was torpedoed by the **USS Grouper**. It is widely accepted that the submarine crew was unaware that the ship was carrying Allied prisoners at the time of the attack. Following the strike, the ship drifted and eventually sank near the Zhoushan archipelago. While the **USS Grouper** is the subject of the engagement, the rescue efforts carried out by local Chinese fishermen in the vicinity remain a central point of discussion in local Hong Kong and mainland historical narratives regarding the conflict.
