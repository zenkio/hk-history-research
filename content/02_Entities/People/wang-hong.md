## Career and Regional Influence
**Wang Hong** (汪鋐) served as the Assistant Administration Commissioner of Guangdong during the **Ming** dynasty. He is most historically recognized for his role in coordinating the maritime defense of the Pearl River Delta during a period of escalating tensions between the Ming state and the expanding Portuguese presence in the region.

## The Battle of Tunmen
In **1521**, **Wang Hong** led the Ming naval forces against the Portuguese fleet in the **Battle of Tunmen** (屯門海戰). The conflict arose following the death of the Ming Emperor Zhengde, which prompted a shift in official policy toward foreign maritime traders. **Wang Hong** is credited with utilizing innovative tactical adaptations, including the adoption of Western-style artillery techniques—specifically the *folangji* (faw-lang-chi) cannons—which were captured and reverse-engineered to bolster Chinese naval defenses.
