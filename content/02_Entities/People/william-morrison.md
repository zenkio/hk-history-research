## Role in Colonial Administration

**William Morrison** (威廉·莫里森) was appointed the first Colonial Surgeon of Hong Kong in **1843**, shortly after the island was formally established as a British Crown Colony. His appointment marked the institutional beginning of the Colonial Surgeon's Office, a department that would later evolve into the modern Department of Health.

Morrison's tenure was defined by the struggle to manage the significant health challenges facing the fledgling colony, most notably high rates of malaria and other tropical diseases.
