## Historical Background
**Yeung Leung-ye** (楊亮節) is historically associated with the final years of the Southern Song dynasty. According to traditional records, he served as a general and court official during the flight of the Song royal court to the south to escape Mongol expansion. He is frequently linked to the defense of the young **Emperor Bing of Song** (宋帝昺) during the dynasty's final stand at the **Battle of Yamen** (崖門海戰) in **1279**.

## Local Veneration and Folklore
In Hong Kong and the New Territories, Yeung is primarily revered as **Hau Wong** (侯王), or 'Marquess King.' While historical records regarding his specific official rank are sparse, local oral traditions suggest that he escorted the royal family through the coastal regions of present-day Hong Kong, including **Kowloon** and **Lantau Island**. Many local communities believe that the shrines dedicated to **Hau Wong** were established to commemorate his loyalty and protection of the child emperor.
