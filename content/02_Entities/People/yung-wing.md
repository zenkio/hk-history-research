## Departure and the Chinese Educational Mission

After graduating from the Morrison school, **Yung Wing** traveled to the United States in **1847**, eventually becoming the first Chinese graduate of **Yale College** in **1854**. Returning to China, he dedicated his career to modernizing the nation's educational and industrial infrastructure through Western learning.

His crowning achievement was the **Chinese Educational Mission**, launched in **1872**, which brought groups of young Chinese students to the United States to study. While late Qing reformers championed Yung's vision as essential self-strengthening, conservative court factions in Beijing grew increasingly suspicious of the students' rapid adoption of Western habits. This political friction ultimately led to the recall of the mission in **1881**.
