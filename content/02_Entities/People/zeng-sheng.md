## Early Life and Guerrilla Command
**Zeng Sheng** (曾生) was born in 1910 in Pingshan, Guangdong. He is best known for his leadership during the **Second Sino-Japanese War**. Following the fall of Hong Kong in December **1941**, he reorganized various anti-Japanese elements into the **East River Column** (東江縱隊), an armed force operating under the influence of the Chinese Communist Party.

## Role in Hong Kong
The **East River Column** operated extensively in the New Territories and across the border in Guangdong. **Zeng Sheng**’s forces were instrumental in the 'Daring Rescue,' a major operation in January **1942** that smuggled over 800 people, including high-profile intellectuals, cultural figures, and Allied pilots, out of occupied Hong Kong into free China.
