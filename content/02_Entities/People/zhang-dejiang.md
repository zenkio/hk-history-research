## Role in Hong Kong Affairs
**Zhang Dejiang** (張德江) served as the Chairman of the Standing Committee of the National People's Congress (**NPCSC**) from **2013** to **2018**. During this tenure, he also chaired the Central Coordination Group for Hong Kong and Macau Affairs. His influence on Hong Kong's political trajectory was most notably marked by his involvement in the constitutional reform debates.

## The 831 Decision
On **August 31, 2014**, the **NPCSC** issued a decision regarding the framework for the **2017** Chief Executive election. This document, often referred to as the "**831 Decision**," mandated that candidates for the Chief Executive must be endorsed by a majority of a broadly representative nominating committee. 

Interpretations of this decision vary significantly. From the perspective of the Beijing government, the decision was framed as a necessary measure to ensure the "patriotism" of candidates and maintain national security within the framework of the Basic Law. Conversely, many pro-democracy figures and activists in Hong Kong criticized the move, arguing that it restricted genuine universal suffrage and contradicted earlier expectations for electoral reform. This disagreement served as a primary catalyst for the **2014** Hong Kong protests, commonly known as the Umbrella Movement.

## Later Years
In **May 2016**, **Zhang Dejiang** visited Hong Kong to attend a forum on the "Belt and Road Initiative." This trip was monitored closely by local media and political observers as a demonstration of Beijing's direct engagement with the city's governance. Following his retirement from the **NPCSC** in **2018**, scholarship on his term remains focused on the central government's shift toward a more proactive stance on the interpretation of the Basic Law and the administration of the Special Administrative Region.
