## Role in Hong Kong Affairs
**Zeng Qinghong** (曾慶紅) served as a high-ranking official in the People's Republic of China, notably as the Vice President from **2003** to **2008**. During this tenure, he held the position of Chairman of the Central Leading Group on Hong Kong and Macau Affairs. In this capacity, he was a key figure in the central government's oversight of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region following the 1997 handover.
