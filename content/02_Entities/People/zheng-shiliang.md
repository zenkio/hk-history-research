## Role in the Early Revolutionary Movement
**Zheng Shiliang** (鄭士良) was a pivotal figure in the early revolutionary activities organized by **Sun Yat-sen** (孫中山) against the Qing Dynasty. A native of Huiyang, Guangdong, Zheng was known for his military leadership and organizational skills. He played an instrumental role in the **Xingzhonghui** (興中會), or Revive China Society, and was deeply involved in the planning of various armed uprisings in the Pearl River Delta region.

## Connection to Hong Kong
Hong Kong served as the essential logistics and communications hub for Zheng's operations. During the **1900** Huizhou Uprising (惠州起義), Zheng relied heavily on the territory to procure weapons, coordinate logistics, and provide a sanctuary for revolutionaries moving across the border. While British colonial authorities in Hong Kong often viewed these activities as a threat to regional stability, they sometimes maintained a cautious distance, allowing for a degree of clandestine revolutionary activity until such actions breached colonial law or diplomatic relations with the Qing state.

## Historical Perspectives
Interpretations of Zheng’s activities vary significantly depending on the historical lens applied. Nationalist historiography often characterizes Zheng as a heroic martyr who sacrificed his life for the cause of modernization and republicanism. Conversely, Qing-era records and certain traditionalist perspectives viewed him and his associates as insurgents or bandits undermining imperial order. Modern scholarship often focuses on the pragmatic use of Hong Kong’s colonial infrastructure, noting how Zheng navigated the unique constraints of the territory to bypass Qing surveillance. The exact extent of support Zheng received from specific local communities in the New Territories remains a subject of ongoing academic research, with some historians noting that local clan networks occasionally intersected with revolutionary supply chains, though the degree of conscious political alignment among these groups remains uncertain.
