## Role in Hong Kong

**Zou Taofen** (鄒韜奮) arrived in Hong Kong in **1937** following the outbreak of the Second Sino-Japanese War. As a leading figure in the National Salvation Association (救國會), his presence transformed the British colony into a temporary center for Chinese intellectual resistance against the Japanese invasion. In Hong Kong, he continued his editorial work, notably launching the publication *Kangdian* (抗戰), which sought to provide news and analysis regarding the war effort to readers in Hong Kong and the Chinese mainland.
