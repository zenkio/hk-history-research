## Origins and Development
Developed by **Amoy Properties** (now **Hang Lung Properties**), **Amoy Gardens** (淘大花園) is a major private housing estate located in **Ngau Tau Kok** (牛頭角), Kowloon. The site was historically occupied by the **Amoy Canning** (淘大食品) factory, which gave the estate its name. Construction occurred in phases between **1981** and **1987**, resulting in a dense complex of 19 residential blocks housing thousands of residents. The development was representative of the rapid urbanization of East Kowloon during the late 20th century.

## The 2003 SARS Outbreak
**Amoy Gardens** gained international attention in **March 2003** during the **Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS)** (嚴重急性呼吸系統綜合症) epidemic. Block E became the primary focal point of the crisis, recording a disproportionately high number of infections. Investigations by the **Department of Health** and the **World Health Organization (WHO)** concluded that the high transmission rate was likely exacerbated by structural factors within the building's drainage system and the proximity of residential units. Specifically, it was hypothesized that the virus spread via aerosolized droplets through dried-up floor drains in bathrooms, a finding that prompted extensive modifications to building codes in Hong Kong.

## Historical Significance and Legacy
For public health officials, the outbreak at **Amoy Gardens** stands as a pivotal event that underscored the vulnerabilities of high-density housing in a modern metropolis. In the immediate aftermath, residents of Block E were subject to a mandatory quarantine, an unprecedented measure in the city’s post-war history. While interpretations of the government's crisis management remain varied—with some critics pointing to delays in the initial response and others emphasizing the rapid scientific mobilization—the event fundamentally transformed Hong Kong's approach to infectious disease control, environmental hygiene, and sanitary engineering. The estate remains a permanent fixture of the city's architectural landscape, serving as a stark reminder of the complexities of urban governance and public safety in a densely populated environment.
