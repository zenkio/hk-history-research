## The 1996 Olympic Games

Atlanta, Georgia, hosted the **1996 Summer Olympic Games** from July 19 to August 4, 1996. For Hong Kong, then still under British administration ahead of the 1997 handover, the games held profound historical and symbolic significance as the territory competed under its own Olympic identity as 'Hong Kong, China'.

## Lee Lai-shan's Historic Victory

During the Atlanta Olympics, windsurfer **Lee Lai-shan** (李麗珊) secured victory in the women's Mistral class on **August 2, 1996**. This achievement marked the first Olympic medal, as well as the first gold medal, in Hong Kong's sporting history. The victory was a source of immense local pride and became a defining cultural moment for the populace during a period of considerable political anxiety surrounding the impending transfer of sovereignty.

## Legacy and Commemoration

The triumph in Atlanta cemented Lee's status as a local sporting icon and led to widespread celebration across the territory. The event is frequently cited by historians and sociologists as a pivotal milestone in the development of a distinct Hong Kong identity, separate from both British colonial rule and mainland political structures.
