## Migration and Clan Origins

Fujian has historically served as a vital point of origin and transit for various lineages that eventually settled in the Hong Kong region. While the most prominent lineages in the New Territories, such as the **Tang (鄧)** and **Liu (劉)** clans, trace their primary ancestral roots further north to Jiangxi or Henan, some local traditions and broader regional genealogies link specific sub-branches and associated communities to migrations originating from or passing through Fujian (福建).

## Maritime Trade and the Great Clearance

During the early Qing dynasty, Fujian played a central role in imperial maritime policies that directly impacted the Hong Kong area. To suppress the anti-Qing resistance of **Koxinga (鄭成功)**, the Qing court implemented the **Great Clearance (遷界令)** in **1661**, forcing the evacuation of coastal populations across Guangdong and Fujian. Following the **Rescission of the Great Clearance** in **1669**, the Qing government actively encouraged resettlement. While this policy primarily targeted domestic migrants from neighboring provinces like Jiangxi, Fujian, and Hunan, it integrated the coastal Hong Kong region into broader southeastern maritime networks under Beijing's control.

## Historiographical Interpretations

Perspectives on the role of Fujianese influences in Hong Kong history vary among historians:

* **Qing / Beijing Official View:** Emphasizes state-led pacification, border security, and the orderly rehabilitation of coastal economies after decades of maritime unrest.
* **Local New Territories Communities:** Focuses primarily on localized lineage continuity, ancestral myths, and the arduous process of reclaiming abandoned lands rather than broader provincial identities.
* **Later Scholarship:** Highlights regional economic integration, noting that southeastern coastal provinces shared overlapping trade routes, dialect ties, and maritime operational networks that transcended strict modern provincial boundaries.

While direct mass settlement from Fujian into the immediate New Territories was secondary to migration from neighboring Guangdong and Jiangxi, Fujian's cultural and economic gravity profoundly influenced the maritime milieu of the South China coast.
