## Background and Development

**Heng Fa Chuen** (杏花邨) is a large-scale private residential estate located in Chai Wan (柴灣) on the eastern end of Hong Kong Island (香港島). The estate was developed by the **MTR Corporation** (Mass Transit Railway Corporation, 地下鐵路公司) in conjunction with **Hang Lung Development** (恒隆開發). Built on reclaimed land, the site originally served as the location for the MTR Chai Wan Depot, above which the residential towers were subsequently constructed starting in the early **1980s**.

## Typhoon Vulnerability and Typhoon Mangkhut

Because of its waterfront location facing the Tathong Channel (藍塘海峽), Heng Fa Chuen has frequently been exposed to severe coastal weather. During **Super Typhoon Mangkhut** (超強颱風山竹) in **September 2018**, the estate gained widespread media attention as storm surges and towering waves battered the promenade, flooding underground carparks and damaging residential blocks. While local residents and management faced significant transport chaos and property damage, the event sparked ongoing public discussions regarding coastal defense measures, property insurance liabilities, and climate change preparedness in high-density urban developments along Hong Kong's shoreline.
