## History and Development

Origins of the institution trace back to the establishment of the Government Trade School in **1937**, located in Wan Chai. It was later renamed the Hong Kong Technical College before moving to its current campus in Hung Hom (紅磡) in **1972**. In **1994**, the institution officially attained full university status and was renamed the Hong Kong Polytechnic University (香港理工大學).

## The 2019 Siege

In **November 2019**, the campus became the site of one of the most dramatic and prolonged confrontations of the **2019 anti-extradition bill protests**. Protesters, largely comprising students and youth, occupied the university grounds, transforming the campus into a fortified stronghold equipped with makeshift weapons.

Interpretations of the siege diverge sharply:

*   **Pro-democracy and local participants** viewed the occupation as a desperate act of self-defense and resistance against perceived police brutality and the erosion of Hong Kong's autonomy.
*   **Hong Kong government and Beijing authorities** characterized the actions as violent rioting and criminal occupation, arguing that police intervention was necessary to restore public order and rule of law.
*   **Later legal and academic scholarship** continues to analyze the event as a watershed moment that marked a turning point in the trajectory of social unrest and subsequent political changes in the territory.
