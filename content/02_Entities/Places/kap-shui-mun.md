## Maritime Significance
**Kap Shui Mun** (汲水門) is a narrow channel located between **Lantau Island** (大嶼山) and **Ma Wan** (馬灣). Historically, it served as one of the primary northern entrances to the waters of the **Pearl River Delta**. Because of its strategic positioning, the channel acted as a natural chokepoint for maritime traffic moving toward **Canton** (廣州).

## The Canton Trade System
During the era of the **Canton System** (廣州十三行), which strictly regulated foreign maritime trade from the **Qing Dynasty** (清朝) onwards, **Kap Shui Mun** functioned as a crucial customs and inspection station. Foreign merchant vessels, including those of the **British East India Company**, were required to pass through checkpoints in this vicinity to register their cargo and pay duties before proceeding to the **Whampoa Anchorage** (黃埔). 

## Differing Historical Perspectives
Interpretations of the area’s history vary across scholarly traditions. **Qing imperial records** prioritize the channel as a defensive perimeter managed by the **Dapeng Battalion** (大鵬營), intended to monitor smuggling and suppress piracy. In contrast, **British colonial historiography** often characterizes the area through the lens of early naval exploration and the mapping of potential deep-water anchorages prior to the formal cession of **Hong Kong Island**. 

Local **New Territories** communities, particularly the **Tanka** (疍家) boat dwellers, maintain a history centered on artisanal fishing and local coastal navigation, often viewing the strait as an ancestral livelihood space rather than a mere geopolitical waypoint. Later scholarship has sought to integrate these perspectives, suggesting that the channel functioned simultaneously as an imperial frontier, a commercial conduit, and a vital resource hub for indigenous maritime populations. While the exact scale of illicit trade passing through the channel during the late 18th century remains a subject of historical debate, its role in the administrative oversight of the **Canton Trade System** is widely acknowledged.
