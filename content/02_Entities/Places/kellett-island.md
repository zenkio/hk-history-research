## Geography and Early History
**Kellett Island** (奇力島) was a small island located off the coast of **East Point** in **Causeway Bay**. It was named after **Captain Henry Kellett**, a British naval officer who surveyed the area in the mid-19th century. During the early colonial period, the island was utilized by the **British Royal Navy** for storage and security purposes, given its strategic position overlooking the eastern entrance of **Victoria Harbour**.
