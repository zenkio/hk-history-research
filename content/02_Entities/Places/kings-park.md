## Origins and Early Development

King's Park (京士柏) is located in the Yau Tsim Mong District of Kowloon. During the early period of British colonial administration, the area was largely undeveloped and utilized for military purposes. The site derived its name from **King Edward VII**, reflecting the British practice of naming topographical features after royalty during the early 20th century.

Historians note that while the land was initially under military control, parts of it were gradually integrated into the civilian sphere as the urban area of Kowloon expanded northward from Tsim Sha Tsui.
