## Introduction
**Leighton Road** (禮頓道) is a prominent east-west thoroughfare situated in the Causeway Bay and Happy Valley districts of Hong Kong Island. Named after a colonial figure, the road has evolved from early British colonial land development into a bustling urban corridor, serving as an important link between Wan Chai and Causeway Bay.

## Connection to Po Leung Kuk
The most historically significant landmark on Leighton Road is the **Po Leung Kuk Permanent Headquarters** (保良局總局). Established in **1878** following a public memorial by local Chinese leaders and the colonial government to combat the abduction of women and children, the institution initially operated elsewhere before moving to its current site on Leighton Road.

## Historiography and Perspectives
