## Development and Location
**Lung Wo Road** (龍和道) is a primary road located in the Central District of **Hong Kong Island**. It was constructed as part of the **Central and Wan Chai Reclamation** project, designed to expand the city's urban footprint along the northern shore. The road runs parallel to the **Victoria Harbour** waterfront, serving as a vital link between the **Central** business district and the **Wan Chai** area. Its creation was part of broader efforts to resolve land scarcity and accommodate the growing transit needs of Hong Kong’s financial core.
