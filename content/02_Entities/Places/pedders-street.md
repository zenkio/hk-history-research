## Introduction and Naming

**Pedder's Street** (畢打街) is located in the heart of the Central district on Hong Kong Island. The street was named after **Lieutenant William Pedder** (畢打), the first Harbour Master of Hong Kong, who arrived with the British forces in **1841**. 

## Connection to the Hongkong Hotel

Pedder's Street is historically notable for its connection to the establishment of the **Hongkong Hotel**, which opened its doors in **1868**. The hotel was conceived by local merchants and entrepreneurs to accommodate the growing number of international travelers, businessmen, and diplomats visiting the British colony. Situated near the waterfront at the time—before subsequent land reclamation pushed the shoreline further north—the hotel quickly became a social hub for the colonial elite.
