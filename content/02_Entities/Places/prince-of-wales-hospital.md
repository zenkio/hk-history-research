## Establishment and Development
The **Prince of Wales Hospital** (威爾斯親王醫院), located in **Shatin** (沙田), opened in **1984**. It serves as the primary regional hospital for the **New Territories East** cluster and is the teaching hospital for the **Faculty of Medicine** at the **Chinese University of Hong Kong** (香港中文大學). Its establishment marked a significant expansion of medical infrastructure in the New Territories to support the rapidly growing population of new towns.

## The 2003 SARS Outbreak
The hospital is most historically significant for its role in the **2003 SARS outbreak**. In **March 2003**, the facility became the primary site of infection after a patient who had stayed at the **Metropole Hotel** in **Kowloon** was admitted to Ward 8A. The subsequent rapid transmission among patients, medical staff, and visitors necessitated a total quarantine of the ward.

Perspectives on the hospital's response vary among scholars and public observers. While the severity of the outbreak was unprecedented, historical assessments remain divided on whether institutional shortcomings or the sheer novelty of the virus were the primary factors in the high rate of infection within the hospital.
