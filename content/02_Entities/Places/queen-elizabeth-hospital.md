## Historical Development
**Queen Elizabeth Hospital** (伊利沙伯醫院) was established to address the acute shortage of hospital beds in post-war Hong Kong. Following the rapid population growth of the 1950s, the Hong Kong government commissioned the facility to serve as the primary general hospital for the Kowloon peninsula. Construction began in the late 1950s, and the hospital was officially opened on **September 6, 1963**, by the Governor of Hong Kong, Sir **Robert Black**.

## Royal Connection

It has been a site of central trauma care, medical training, and public health policy implementation. The hospital's architecture and its proximity to the **Kowloon** transport hub reflect the mid-20th-century urban planning priorities of the colonial administration.
