## Historical Overview
Opened in **1937**, Queen Mary Hospital (瑪麗醫院) was established by the British colonial government to replace the aging Government Civil Hospital. Located in Pok Fu Lam, the hospital was named after **Queen Mary of Teck**, the consort of King George V. It has since evolved into the flagship medical institution for the **University of Hong Kong** (香港大學), serving as the hub for medical education and specialized clinical research in the territory.

## Medical Significance and Cultural Context
As the tertiary referral center for the Hong Kong West Cluster, the hospital is internationally recognized for its advancements in liver transplantation and oncology. Due to its status as a major medical center, it has often been the site of medical treatment for prominent public figures.

On **April 1, 2003**, the hospital gained widespread public attention following the death of the Cantopop icon and film star **Leslie Cheung** (張國榮). After falling from the Mandarin Oriental Hotel, Cheung was rushed to Queen Mary Hospital, where he was pronounced dead at the age of 46.
