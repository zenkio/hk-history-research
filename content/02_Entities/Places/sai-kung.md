## Historical Significance
Sai Kung (西貢), traditionally known as a region of scattered coastal villages and market towns, holds a central place in the study of regional prehistory. The area is particularly noted for the discovery of **Coarse Cord-Marked Pottery** (粗繩紋陶), which scholars associate with the late Neolithic to Bronze Age periods. These artifacts provide essential evidence of early maritime settlements along the eastern coastline of Hong Kong.
