## Geography and Early History
Shek Kong (**石崗**) is located in the central New Territories near the base of Tai Mo Shan (**大帽山**). Historically, the area was composed of traditional Punti (**本地**) villages, whose livelihoods were primarily agricultural. The region served as a crossroads between the northern New Territories and the southern coastal areas.

## The Six-Day War (1899)
The most significant historical event associated with Shek Kong occurred during the **Six-Day War** of **1899**. Following the British lease of the New Territories, local clans organized armed resistance against the colonial administration. Shek Kong became a focal point for this conflict; it was here that the British military, under the command of **Major-General Gascoigne**, confronted the main force of the village militias. Historical interpretations of this event vary: British colonial records often characterized the campaign as a 'pacification' effort necessary to establish rule, while local New Territories communities and later nationalist scholarship frequently emphasize the resistance as a defense of ancestral land and sovereign integrity against foreign annexation.

## Military Significance
Following the stabilization of the New Territories, the British military recognized the strategic value of the flat plains in Shek Kong. During the mid-20th century, the **Shek Kong Airfield** was constructed, serving as a vital facility for the British Armed Forces until the transition of sovereignty in **1997**. The airfield remains a landmark of the colonial military presence. Today, the site serves the People's Liberation Army (PLA) Hong Kong Garrison. The transition of the base has been viewed differently by various observers: some scholars highlight it as a symbol of the end of colonial geography, while local residents often reflect on the shift from a rural-military buffer zone to a strictly regulated facility under new governance. The area continues to balance its traditional village heritage with its long-standing role as a controlled military installation.
