**Tai Wan** (大灣) is located on the eastern coast of **Lamma Island** and represents one of the most important archaeological sites in the region. Excavations conducted primarily in the **1950s** and **1960s** revealed an extensive cultural sequence spanning from the **Neolithic period** through the **Bronze Age**. The site is particularly noted for its abundance of pottery and stone tools, which indicate that the area served as a center for prehistoric life.
