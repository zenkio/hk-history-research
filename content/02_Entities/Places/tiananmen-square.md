## Historical Significance
Located in Beijing, **Tiananmen Square** (天安門廣場) is a site of immense political symbolism. For Hong Kong, the square is primarily associated with the **1989 Democracy Movement** and the subsequent military crackdown on **June 4, 1989**. These events triggered widespread protests in Hong Kong, marking a turning point in the territory’s political consciousness regarding its future under the **Sino-British Joint Declaration**.

## Impact on Hong Kong Identity
In the weeks leading up to **June 4, 1989**, Hong Kong residents held massive rallies in solidarity with students occupying the square. These demonstrations, involving over a million participants, created a localized sense of responsibility toward China's democratic development. Following the crackdown, the events in **Tiananmen Square** became a central pillar of Hong Kong's annual political culture, characterized by decades of vigils held in **Victoria Park**.

## Diverging Interpretations
The legacy of the square remains a subject of differing historical perspectives. Official narratives from Beijing emphasize the maintenance of social stability and the restoration of order as necessary for national development. Conversely, many in Hong Kong—and various international scholars—have framed the events as a seminal struggle for civil liberties. 

## Connection to Hong Kong Commemoration
The significance of the square's events was physically marked in Hong Kong for years by the **Pillar of Shame** (國殤之柱), a sculpture dedicated to the victims of the **1989** crackdown. Its removal from the **University of Hong Kong** in **December 2021** sparked debates regarding the interpretation of historical memory, the autonomy of academic institutions, and the shifting political landscape of Hong Kong under the **National Security Law**. While some view the removal as a necessary alignment with the legal and political environment of the city, others interpret it as a significant shift in the capacity for public commemoration of the **1989** events.
