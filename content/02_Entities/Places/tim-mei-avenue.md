## Geography and Location

Tim Mei Avenue (添美道) is located in the Admiralty (金鐘) area of Hong Kong Island, situated directly adjacent to the Central Government Complex (政府總部) and the Legislative Council Complex (立法綜合大樓). Because of its strategic proximity to the seat of local governance, the avenue has historically functioned as a primary focal point for public assemblies, petitions, and political protests.

## Role in Political Protests

The street gained significant prominence during modern sociopolitical movements in Hong Kong. Later scholarship and local media analysis often highlight Tim Mei Avenue as part of the broader 'civic square' geography that physically connects citizens with executive and legislative power. These differing interpretations reflect ongoing tensions regarding freedom of assembly and the use of public space in post-colonial Hong Kong.
