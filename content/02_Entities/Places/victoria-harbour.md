## Geographic and Strategic Importance

**Victoria Harbour** (維多利亞港) is a natural landform situated between Hong Kong Island (香港島) and the Kowloon Peninsula (九龍半島). Its deep, sheltered waters provided a strategic anchorage that played a central role in British colonial ambitions following the First Opium War.

## Colonial Expansion and Maritime Infrastructure

The harbor's shores witnessed key milestones in colonial administration and territorial acquisition. Following the **Convention of Peking** (**北京條約**) in **1860**, which ceded the Kowloon Peninsula, the expanse of the harbor fell entirely under British control. As maritime traffic grew, large-scale engineering projects reshaped the waterfront, notably marked by the **Launch of the Praya Reclamation Scheme** in the late 19th century to expand usable land.

## Natural Disasters and Urban Growth

The vitality of the harbor was frequently tested by extreme weather. **The Great Typhoon of 1874** and **The Great Typhoon of 1906** caused catastrophic destruction to shipping and infrastructure within the anchorage, prompting ongoing debates between colonial authorities and local communities regarding maritime safety and disaster response.

## Historiographical Interpretations

Interpretations of the harbor's development diverge significantly across historiographical traditions. British colonial accounts historically emphasized the transformation of the harbor through engineering and free-trade policies into a premier port of the British Empire. In contrast, mainland Chinese scholarship often frames these developments through the lens of imperial encroachment, highlighting how the harbor facilitated foreign control over Chinese territory. Local Hong Kong and New Territories communities have increasingly emphasized the indigenous maritime heritage, labor contributions, and the displacement caused by continuous reclamation projects along the waterfront.

## Timeline

- **1874**: The Great Typhoon of 1874 strikes the anchorage, causing severe damage to shipping and infrastructure.
- **1906**: Another catastrophic typhoon hits Victoria Harbour, resulting in significant loss of life and maritime vessels.
