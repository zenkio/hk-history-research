## Historical Development
Originally a small coastal area known as **Ha Wan** (下環), Wanchai was one of the earliest areas settled by the British after the founding of **Victoria City**. The name **Wanchai** (灣仔), meaning "small bay," refers to its original geography before the massive land reclamation projects that expanded the district northward. Throughout the late **19th century**, the area developed into a residential quarter for the working class and a commercial district serving the harbor.

## Religious and Educational Institutions

The establishment of the **St. Francis Xavier School** (聖芳濟書院) is a notable example of the role religious organizations played in shaping the educational landscape of early Hong Kong. These institutions often served as bridges between the local Chinese population and colonial authorities, though the extent of their influence on cultural assimilation remains a subject of ongoing historical discussion. Some scholars argue that these schools were instrumental in modernizing local education, while others suggest they functioned as extensions of colonial power structures.
