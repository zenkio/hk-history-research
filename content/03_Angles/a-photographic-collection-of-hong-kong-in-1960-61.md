A collection of 20 colour slides, discovered in a charity shop in **Seaford, East Sussex**, provides a visual record of **Hong Kong** between **1960** and **1961**. According to the archival website **Gwulo**, the slides capture diverse scenes, including **Kai Tak Airport**, the **Star Ferry**, **Mong Kok**, and various rural landscapes. These images offer insight into urban and social conditions in the colony during the early **1960s**. The source notes that the slides serve as a primary reference for historians examining the development of local landmarks and daily life during that period.
