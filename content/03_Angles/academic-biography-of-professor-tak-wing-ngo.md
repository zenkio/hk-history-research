**Tak-Wing Ngo** is a distinguished scholar specializing in the political economy of East Asia. He earned his PhD from **SOAS London** and has held academic appointments at **Leiden University** and **Erasmus University Rotterdam**. According to the **Hong Kong History Centre**, **Ngo** currently serves as a Professor of Political Science at the **University of Macau**. Furthermore, he maintains editorial roles for the publications **China Information** and the **Journal of Contemporary Asia**.
