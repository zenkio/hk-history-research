A private collection of color slides purchased in Hong Kong around **1986** provides visual evidence of the city's urban development throughout the mid-to-late 20th century. According to the author **gw** on **Gwulo**, these slides were analyzed to establish specific timelines for various locations, including **Central**, **Causeway Bay**, and **The University of Hong Kong**. The photographic evidence documents significant architectural milestones, such as the construction of the **Mandarin Hotel** in **1963** and the inauguration of the **MTR** system in **1979**. By cross-referencing these images with historical maps and building completion records, researchers successfully narrowed down the origin dates for individual frames to ranges between **1962** and **1979**. The collection serves as a primary visual resource for understanding the rapid transformation of the Hong Kong landscape during this period.
