As of **2026**, **Anne Lovejoy** served as a Senior Archivist at the **Hong Kong History Centre**. She collaborated with the **University of Bristol** to manage and develop the institution's specialized **Hong Kong Collections**. According to the **Hong Kong History Centre** website, **Lovejoy** brought extensive experience in leading large-scale cataloguing and digitisation projects. Her professional background also included previous work in the historic environment sector, reflecting a specialized interest in architectural history.
