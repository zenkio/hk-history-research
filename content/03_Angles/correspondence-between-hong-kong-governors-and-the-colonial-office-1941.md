Between **1941** and the eve of the Japanese invasion of Hong Kong, diplomatic correspondence was exchanged between Hong Kong Governors **Sir Geoffry Northcote** and **Sir Mark Young** and the British Colonial Office representative **Sir Edward Gent**. The letters, sourced from the National Archives file **CO/129/590/14**, document administrative concerns, including the **Cressall Commission** on government corruption and the regional geopolitical situation involving the Nationalist government in Chungking. **Sir Mark Young** reflected on the political outlook of local cadet officers and the removal of **Norman Lockhart Smith** from his post as Colonial Secretary. The documents also provide biographical context for these colonial officials and their subsequent careers during the Second World War and the early post-war era in Malaya.
