The Gwulo website documented several historical records in **August 2026**, including the biography of **Phyllis Nolasco Da Silva**, who served as a British Army Aid Group (BAAG) operative in **Kwongchowwan** during the **1941/1945** Japanese occupation. The project initiated a crowdsourced effort to transcribe a **Shamshuipo POW camp** wartime diary using AI-assisted tools. Additional archival updates included research on the Protestant elite in **Hong Kong**, the history of **King’s College**, and various photographic contributions depicting city infrastructure and landmarks from the 1930s through the 1970s. These materials, reported by **David** on Gwulo, highlight ongoing efforts to preserve personal accounts and institutional histories of **Hong Kong**.
