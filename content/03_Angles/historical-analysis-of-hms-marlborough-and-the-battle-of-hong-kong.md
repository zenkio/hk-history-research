Launched in **1855**, **HMS Marlborough** served as a prominent **First Rate** battleship and flagship of the Mediterranean Fleet until **1864**. The vessel was later converted into an accommodation hulk known as **Vernon II** before ultimately sinking while being towed for scrapping in **1924**. In addition to naval history, the source contains a correspondence regarding the **1941 Battle of Hong Kong**. Author **Philip Cracknell** discusses specific tactical movements involving the **Royal Scots**, the **Royal Rifles**, and the **Shoji Butai** during the defense of the territory. These exchanges analyze the strategic challenges faced by Allied forces near **Repulse Bay** and the **Advanced Dressing Station**.
