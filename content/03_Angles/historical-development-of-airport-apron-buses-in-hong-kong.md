The **Industrial History of Hong Kong Group** identifies airport apron buses as a component of the territory's forgotten transportation history. These specialized vehicles were essential for the ground operations of aviation infrastructure throughout the mid-to-late 20th century. While specific deployment dates and operational models remain documented within the group's wider archives, this subject forms part of the broader study of industrial and utility transport. The source highlights the importance of preserving these elements of **Hong Kong**'s industrial heritage for future study.
