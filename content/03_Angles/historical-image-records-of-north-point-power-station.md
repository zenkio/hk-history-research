The **North Point Power Station** was a significant industrial facility in Hong Kong, documented through various photographs spanning from the late **1940s** to the early **1970s**. According to the **Industrial History of Hong Kong Group**, these records include images of a **1948** Shell oil tanker operation and chimney demolition work observed in the **1960s** and **1970s**. The source materials, provided by contributors such as **IDJ**, highlight the site's evolution over several decades. These visual documents serve as an important reference for the industrial development of the North Point area.
