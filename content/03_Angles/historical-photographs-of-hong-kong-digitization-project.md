The **Hong Kong History Centre** launched the Historical Photographs of Hong Kong (**HPHK**) platform in **2026** to publish donated photographic materials held by the **University of Bristol Library**. The collection features vernacular photography, including works by engineer **Nigel Spry**, who documented scenes such as **Shek O** beach during his residence from **1981** to **1984** and **1986** to **1989**. According to the platform administrators, these images serve as valuable records of private family life and street scenes in the city. The archive intends to provide a long-term repository for visual records that offer insight into Hong Kong's history.
