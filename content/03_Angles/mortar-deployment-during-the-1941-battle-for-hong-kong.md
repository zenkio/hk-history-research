During the **December 1941** Battle for Hong Kong, British and Canadian forces faced significant equipment shortages regarding infantry mortars. According to the **London Gazette** report by **Major General C. M. Maltby**, the garrison lacked adequate training, specialized carrying equipment, and ammunition until just before hostilities commenced. Mortars were often manhandled into battle positions, as noted in the **2/14 Punjab Regiment** operations report. Battlefield evidence, such as recovered nose caps, indicates their deployment at locations including **Notting Hill**, **Tai Tam Fork Battery**, and **Mount Cameron**. Historical accounts from the **Royal Rifles of Canada** war diary document instances where units struggled with ineffective shelling or were forced to abandon mortar equipment during retreats.
