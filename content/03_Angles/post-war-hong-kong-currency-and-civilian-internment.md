Following the **1945** Japanese surrender, authorities prepared to overprint Chinese and Japanese banknotes as Hong Kong Dollars, though these were ultimately rendered unnecessary by the arrival of new British currency. According to **Francis Braun**, a Hungarian internee, civilian life in occupied Hong Kong was characterized by significant hardship, including internment at **Stanley Prison** starting in **1941**. **Braun** also observed the intensification of American air raids and the control of information via Japanese-run media. The review by **Philip Cracknell** evaluates **Braun**'s personal account of these events while noting the specific focus on failed currency reforms.
