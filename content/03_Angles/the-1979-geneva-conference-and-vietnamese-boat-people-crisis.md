Beginning in the early **1970s**, Hong Kong experienced a significant influx of refugees fleeing Vietnam, placing immense strain on local resources. According to **Professor Ray Yep** of the **University of Bristol**, the international community provided insufficient aid, resulting in Hong Kong being designated as a port of first asylum. The **1979 Geneva Conference** served as a critical diplomatic attempt to address this humanitarian crisis and the resulting political challenges. The **Hong Kong History Centre** highlights this period as a pivotal moment in the territory's social and administrative history.
