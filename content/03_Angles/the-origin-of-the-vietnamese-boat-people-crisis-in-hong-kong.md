Beginning in the early **1970s**, Hong Kong experienced a significant influx of refugees fleeing Vietnam, which placed immense strain on the territory's resources. According to **Professor Ray Yep** from the **University of Bristol**, the international community provided only limited support during this period. Consequently, Hong Kong was designated as a port of first asylum, forcing the local government to manage the humanitarian crisis largely on its own. This era remains a critical study of migration and administrative burden in **Hong Kong** history.
