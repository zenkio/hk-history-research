The **Historical Photographs of Hong Kong (HPHK)** platform was utilized to facilitate the preservation and exploration of personal family histories within the global diaspora. According to **Vivian Kong**, archival photographs from the **Nigel Spry Collection**—specifically those dating from **1982**, **1983**, and **1984**—served as effective prompts to elicit oral histories from elders. The study highlighted how visual records of festivals and daily street life, such as depictions of Mid-Autumn celebrations or traditional baby carriers in local markets, helped individuals recollect details of their upbringing. This methodological approach sought to bridge the communication gap between generations when direct questioning proved challenging.
