Beginning in the **early 1970s**, Hong Kong faced a significant humanitarian crisis due to a massive influx of refugees from **Vietnam**. According to **Professor Ray Yep** from the **University of Bristol**, the territory was designated as a 'port of first asylum' while receiving limited support from the international community. The lecture series examines how **Hong Kong** navigated the conflicting geopolitical interests of the **United Kingdom**, characterized as indifferent, and the **United States**, which exerted significant pressure on the colony. This period highlighted the immense administrative and social burdens placed on the territory during the height of the **Vietnamese boat people crisis**.
